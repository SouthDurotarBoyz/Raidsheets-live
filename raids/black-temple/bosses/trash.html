BLACK TEMPLE TRASH: Less punishing than Tier 5 trash, but there is considerably more of it. Many packs roam, so positioning matters: pull packs back and use line of sight where possible. Start conservatively with crowd control, then reduce CC if packs are dying easily.

Trash Before Naj'entus

Coilskar Sea-Caller

MOVE
  • Forked Lightning30-yard frontal cone that hits up to 5 players for around 2,400 Nature.
  • HurricaneAoE placed at a player’s location for 20 seconds; deals around 1,500–2,000 Nature every 3 seconds and slows attack speed by 67%.
  • GeyserHits a random target and players within 10 yards for around 2,000 Frost, knocking them back and up.

How to handlePoint the frontal cone away from the raid. Move out of Hurricane immediately and avoid stacking for Geyser.

Aqueous Lord

FACE AWAY
  • Vile SlimeRandom target; lasts 24 seconds, deals 500 Nature every 3 seconds, and reduces damage and healing done by 50%.
  • Crashing WaveFrontal cone with an enormous 40-yard range; around 2,300 Nature.

How to handleTank faces the Aqueous Lord away from the raid.

Leviathan

SPREAD
  • Poison SpitRandom target; around 1,700 initial Nature, then around 2,200 every 5 seconds to the target and players within 8 yards.
  • Tail SweepRear cone damage and knockback.
  • Debilitating SprayNo direct damage; reduces damage and healing output by 50% for 10 seconds on the target and players within 8 yards.
  • Mortal StrikeTank-facing melee pressure that reduces healing received.

How to handleSpread the raid. Melee attack from the side: the front is dangerous and standing directly behind risks Tail Sweep. A paired Wrangler can Frenzy and empower this Leviathan.

Coilskar Wrangler

CONTROL CLEAVE
  • Lightning ProdChain-style Nature attack centered through its target and bouncing to nearby targets.
  • CleaveHits nearby frontal/melee targets.
  • Frenzy LeviathanCan Frenzy and empower a nearby Leviathan.

How to handleTanks stack appropriately for Cleave while other melee stay at safe/max melee positioning. Spread to limit Lightning Prod chains and keep the Wrangler–Leviathan interaction visible.

Aqueous Spawn

KILL BEFORE LORD
  • Sludge NovaCastable AoE that can be stunned or interrupted/kicked; its stacking damage punishes clustered players.
  • Merge10-second channel that heals the Aqueous Lord for 2% maximum health per second.

How to handleKILL AQUEOUS SPAWN BEFORE AQUEOUS LORD: Merge heals/empowers the Lord. Melee should avoid stacking into repeated AoE on the post-Naj’entus pull. Engineering Mana Bombs are optional if available.

Coilskar General

ISOLATE FROM CC
  • Free FriendRemoves crowd-control effects from allied mobs.
  • Booming VoiceIncreases the General’s and nearby allies’ attack and casting speed for 30 seconds.

How to handleThe General can break allied mobs out of CC. When using CC, consider tanking it outside the main stacked group and out of line of sight of controlled mobs.

Coilskar Harpooner

SHEEP / CC
  • Spear ThrowRanged multi-target attack hitting up to 10 players for around 1,200 Physical each.
  • Harpooner's MarkForces Dragon Turtles to attack the marked target.
  • Hooked NetImmobilizes a random target for 6 seconds and deals around 2,750 Physical.

How to handleCan be Polymorphed. When paired with a Turtle, crowd-control one of the two.

Coilskar Soothsayer

INTERRUPT HEALS
  • Holy NovaDamages nearby enemies and heals nearby friendly targets.
  • RestorationLarge heal on an ally followed by repeated healing over its duration.

How to handleThis healer/caster’s healing cannot be ignored: interrupt or control Holy Nova and Restoration.

Dragon Turtle

HIBERNATE / CC
  • Water SpitRandom-target Frost damage.
  • Shell ShieldReflects 50% of magic damage while active and reduces attack speed and casting rate by 25% for 12 seconds.
  • Harpooner’s MarkA Harpooner can force the Turtle to attack a newly marked target.

How to handleCaster DPS stop attacking into Shell Shield. Druids can Hibernate the Turtle; when paired with a Harpooner, crowd-control either one so both are not active together.

Coilskar Sea-Caller

MOVE
  • Forked Lightning30-yard frontal cone that hits up to 5 players for around 2,400 Nature.
  • HurricaneAoE placed at a player’s location for 20 seconds; deals around 1,500–2,000 Nature every 3 seconds and slows attack speed by 67%.

How to handlePoint the frontal cone away from the raid. Move out of Hurricane immediately and avoid stacking for Geyser.

Trash Before Supremus

Bonechewer Taskmaster

CONTROL WORKERS
  • FuryIncreases its own size and damage by 10%; can stack multiple times.
  • Enrage WorkersCan enrage nearby Bonechewer Workers, roughly increasing their health by 300% and damage by 200%.

How to handleControl the Taskmaster’s interaction before treating Workers as routine trash.

Bonechewer Worker

LOW PRIORITY

How to handleStraightforward and low priority once the Taskmaster’s Worker enrage interaction is controlled.

Illidari Fearbringer

SOLO PULL

  • Illidari FlamesFrontal cone dealing 2,850–3,150 Fire per second.
  • War StompRaid-dangerous stun and damage across a large effective area.

How to handleFight one at a time where possible. Point the frontal away from the raid, move immediately from Rain of Chaos, and respect War Stomp’s wide stun area.

Dragonmaw Sky Stalker

DRAG DOWN
  • Immolation ArrowTargets a random player with Fire damage and leaves a persistent fire patch.

How to handleThe threat target runs away to drag the airborne mob down. Hunters may optionally use Distracting Shot to help move it while the tank positions for pickup. Once controlled, it is not especially threatening.

Dragonmaw Wyrmcaller

FACE AWAY
  • CleaveMulti-target frontal attack for 110% normal melee damage.
  • FelfireMelee proc for 925–1,075 extra Fire with an 8-second internal cooldown.
  • JabHits and knocks the tank back.
  • FixateMay fixate a random enemy.

How to handleTanks manage multi-target Cleave positioning and brace for Jab knockback. Be ready to move when Fixate changes its focus.

Dragonmaw Wind Reaver

DRAG DOWN
  • Doom Bolt / Doom BlastDoom Bolt hits its target; Doom Blast splashes Shadow damage around that target.
  • FreezeAoE Frost Nova-style damage and control pressure, immobilizing nearby players.
  • FireballDeals initial Fire damage followed by a short DoT.

How to handleThis airborne mob must be dragged down: its threat target outranges it and moves away until tanks and melee can connect.

Ashtongue Packs

Feral Spirits have enormous aggro range. All Ashtongue mobs become friendly once Shade of Akama is defeated.

Ashtongue Feral Spirit

HUGE AGGRO
  • Charge FrenzyRandomly charges enemies every 2 seconds.
  • Spirit BondHeals 5% health per second, roughly 5,000 health per second.
  • Stealth DetectionCan detect and see through Stealth.

How to handleExtremely large aggro radius: give it a wide berth to avoid body-pulls. It sees through Stealth and charges random enemies.

Storm Fury

MOVE
  • Lightning CloudNature ground AoE with around 1,900 initial damage, followed by around 2,100 Nature every 3 seconds to players remaining within roughly 10 yards; lasts approximately 15 seconds.
  • BlinkRepositions and causes nearby AoE pressure when it moves.

How to handleMOVE OUT immediately. Do not remain in Lightning Cloud. Be ready to move when Storm Fury Blinks; do not assume the area around its new position is safe.

Ashtongue Mystic

KILL TARGET 1
  • Chain HealAround 16,000 initial healing and jumps to up to 3 nearby targets.
  • Flame ShockInitial Fire damage plus a periodic effect.
  • Frost ShockFrost damage and a slow.
  • BloodlustDispellable magic buff granting hostile mobs 35% increased attack and casting rate for 20 seconds.
  • Searing TotemRepeatedly attacks with Fire damage.
  • Windfury TotemBuffs nearby enemies with Windfury.

How to handlePriority target. Dispel Bloodlust quickly; Mass Dispel is specifically useful when appropriate. Immediately swap to and kill Windfury Totem, and interrupt Chain Heal.

Ashtongue Stormcaller

CASTER
  • Chain LightningNature spell that chains through nearby targets.
  • Lightning ShieldReactive lightning protection around the caster.
  • Lightning BoltDirect Nature cast.

How to handleControl its casts and avoid unnecessary chaining.

Ashtongue Primalist

KILL TARGET 2
  • Wyvern StingSleeps a random target for 12 seconds, then applies a Nature DoT.
  • Multi-ShotHits up to 3 targets.
  • Sweeping Wing ClipClose-range immobilize affecting players within 5 yards.

How to handleNext major priority after Mystics: its high damage makes it dangerous. Position its back toward a wall where practical so movement does not become chaotic while nearby players are immobilized.

Ashtongue Battlelord

FACE AWAY
  • CleaveThreatens nearby frontal players.
  • Concussion BlowStuns the tank for 5 seconds and can compromise threat.
  • Enrage / FrenzyIncreases damage per hit and attack speed.

How to handleTank it outside and facing away from the stacked raid group. Expect the tank stun and protect threat.

Ashtongue Stalker

POISON CLEANSE

  • BlindDisorients its target.
  • Mind-numbing PoisonPoison pressure that slows casting.
  • Deadly / Instant PoisonUses damaging poison attacks, including Instant Poison and deadly poison pressure.

How to handleExpect the stealth opener, keep poison cleanses ready, and control Blind.

Patrol

This dangerous patrol includes the Illidari Nightlord with Heartseeker/Boneslicer support. Let it patrol away and skip the pack where the route allows.

Illidari Nightlord

SKIP PATROL
  • Curse of MendingGives attacks/effects roughly a 20% chance to heal the cursed target.
  • FearBrief fear lasting about 3 seconds according to raid notes.
  • HellfireAoE damage around the Nightlord.
  • ShadowfiendsCan summon Shadowfiend adds.

How to handleSkip this dangerous patrol where the route allows. If engaged, control the curse, fear, surrounding Hellfire, and summoned adds.

Illidari Heartseeker

BANISH
  • Curse of the BleakheartThree-minute curse on random targets that periodically stuns; decursable.
  • Rapid ShotEight-second rapid channel; Incapacitate effects can stop it.
  • Skeleton ShotHeavy Shadow DoT; if its target dies, a Fallen Ally spawns.

How to handleRanged positioning makes this mob difficult to maneuver. Banish it when assigned; when active, point/control Rapid Shot away from the group where practical.

Illidari Boneslicer

TANK SWAP
  • GougeFrontal/AoE-like effect that can disable the active tank and players within 8 yards.
  • Cloak of ShadowsMakes it temporarily difficult to hit with spells.
  • ShadowstepTeleports to a target.

How to handleFace it away from the raid and other tanks. Another tank must be ready to taunt on Gouge. Raid-specific option: a Paladin tank may place a sacrifice effect on another tank/player to help break its own Gouge where applicable.
