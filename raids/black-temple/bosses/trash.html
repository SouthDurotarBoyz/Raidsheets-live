Trash Before Naj'entus

Leviathan

SPREAD
  • Poison SpitRandom target; around 1,700 initial Nature, then around 2,200 every 5 seconds to the target and players within 8 yards.
  • Tail SweepRear cone damage and knockback.
  • Debilitating SprayNo direct damage; reduces damage and healing output by 50% for 10 seconds on the target and players within 8 yards.

How to handleSpread the raid. Melee attack from the side to avoid the rear Tail Sweep and frontal or clustered Debilitating Spray issues.

Coilskar Wrangler

SPREAD
  • Lightning ProdNature chain-style attack that bounces to nearby targets.
  • CleaveFrontal attack.

How to handleSpread to limit Lightning Prod chaining. The tank faces the Wrangler away from melee and the raid.

Dragon Turtle

HIBERNATE / CC
  • Water SpitRandom-target Frost damage.
  • Shell ShieldReflects 50% of magic damage taken; reduces attack speed and casting rate by 25% for 12 seconds.

How to handleCaster DPS must notice Shell Shield and avoid killing themselves into the reflection. Druids can Hibernate the Turtle. When paired with a Harpooner, crowd-control either one so both are not active together.

Coilskar Harpooner

SHEEP / CC
  • Spear ThrowRanged multi-target attack hitting up to 10 players for around 1,200 Physical each.
  • Harpooner's MarkForces Dragon Turtles to attack the marked target.
  • Hooked NetImmobilizes a random target for 6 seconds and deals around 2,750 Physical.

How to handleCan be Polymorphed. When paired with a Turtle, crowd-control one of the two.

Coilskar General

LOS GENERAL
  • Free FriendRemoves crowd-control effects from allied mobs.
  • Booming VoiceBuffs the General and allies with increased attack and casting speed for 30 seconds.

How to handleIf controlling a Turtle or Harpooner, pull or position the General out of line of sight of those enemies so Free Friend cannot repeatedly remove their CC.

Aqueous Lord

FACE AWAY
  • Vile SlimeRandom target; lasts 24 seconds, deals 500 Nature every 3 seconds, and reduces damage and healing done by 50%.
  • Crashing WaveFrontal cone with an enormous 40-yard range; around 2,300 Nature.

How to handleTank faces the Aqueous Lord away from the raid.

Aqueous Spawn

KILL QUICKLY
  • Sludge NovaHits the target and players within 8 yards for around 1,500–2,000 initial Nature, then 1,000 every 3 seconds; slows movement 20% for 10 seconds and the DoT stacks.
  • Merge10-second channel that heals the Aqueous Lord for 2% maximum health per second.

How to handleKill quickly: Spawns have low health, heavy AoE, and can heal the Lord with Merge.

Coilskar Sea-Caller

MOVE
  • Forked Lightning30-yard frontal that hits up to 5 players for around 2,400 Nature.
  • HurricanePlaced at a player's location for 20 seconds; deals around 1,500–2,000 Nature every 3 seconds and slows attack speed by 67%.
  • GeyserHits a random target and players within 10 yards for around 2,000 Frost and knocks them into the air.

How to handleMove out of Hurricane immediately. Tank keeps Forked Lightning away from unnecessary targets; avoid stacking for Geyser.

Trash Before Supremus

Bonechewer Taskmaster

WATCH FURY

Bonechewer Worker

TANK STUN
  • Throw PickIts only special ability: disarms itself, deals around 800 Physical, and applies Throbbing Stun for 3 seconds.

Dragonmaw Sky Stalker

DRAG DOWN
  • Immolation ArrowDeals 3,700–4,300 initial Fire and leaves persistent burning ground that deals additional Fire every 3 seconds to players standing in it.

How to handleThe Sky Stalker remains airborne. Whoever has threat must run away from it; as it follows, it gradually descends until melee and tanks can engage.

Dragonmaw Wyrmcaller

FACE AWAY
  • CleaveFrontal attack for 110% normal melee damage.
  • FelfireMelee proc for 925–1,075 extra Fire with an 8-second internal cooldown.
  • JabHits the primary threat target for around 2,300 Physical and knocks them back.

How to handleFace the frontal Cleave away and position for Jab knockback.

Illidari Fearbringer

SOLO PULL
  • Rain of ChaosGround AoE dealing 3,200–3,800 Fire every 3 seconds.
  • Illidari FlamesFrontal cone dealing 2,850–3,150 Fire per second.
  • War StompShort-range stun and damage.

How to handleFight Fearbringers one at a time where possible, and watch for roaming mobs and patrols.

Dragonmaw Wind Reaver

DRAG DOWN
  • Doom BoltDeals 2,250–2,750 Shadow to the primary target; Doom Blast splashes 1,200–1,500 Shadow within 12 yards.
  • FreezeDeals 2,200–2,500 Frost in a 15-yard radius and immobilizes for 8 seconds.
  • FireballDeals 1,500–2,000 initial Fire, then 200 Fire each second for 3 seconds.

How to handleUse the same airborne handling as the Sky Stalker: the threat target runs away to drag it down until melee and tanks can connect.

Trash Before Shade of Akama

Illidari Demons

Illidari Defiler

MOVE
  • Rain of ChaosGround AoE with around 3,400 Fire ticks; move immediately.
  • Fel ImmolateAround 2,000 initial Fire plus 500 periodic Fire.
  • Curse of AgonyCurse DoT whose damage increases over time.
  • BanishDisables a random player for 15 seconds; the banished player cannot take damage.

How to handleMove immediately from Rain of Chaos.

Illidari Centurion

FACE AWAY
  • Sonic Strike35-yard frontal cone that silences for 5 seconds.
  • CleaveFrontal attack.

How to handleTank faces it completely away from the raid. Avoid using a Paladin tank when possible because the Silence is awkward for Paladins; this is a recommendation, not a requirement.

Illidari Boneslicer

CONTROL
  • GougeAoE that incapacitates players within 8 yards.
  • Cloak of ShadowsMakes it temporarily difficult to hit with spells.
  • ShadowstepTeleports to a target.

Illidari Heartseeker

BANISH
  • Curse of the BleakheartThree-minute curse on random targets that periodically stuns; decursable.
  • Rapid ShotEight-second channel making four attacks per second; Incapacitate effects such as Rogue Gouge, Paladin Repentance, or Engineering Grenades can stop it.
  • Skeleton ShotDeals 4,000 initial Shadow and 1,500 Shadow every 3 seconds for 30 seconds; if the target dies, a Fallen Ally spawns.

How to handleHeartseekers can be Banished. Use the existing generic Banish assignment; control Rapid Shot with an Incapacitate.

Ashtongue Packs

Feral Spirits have enormous aggro range. All Ashtongue mobs become friendly once Shade of Akama is defeated.

Ashtongue Battlelord

TANK STUN
  • CleaveFrontal attack.
  • Concussion BlowMinor damage and a 5-second tank stun that can compromise tank threat generation.
  • EnrageIncreases damage per hit and attack speed.

How to handleExpect the tank stun and protect threat.

Ashtongue Feral Spirit

HUGE AGGRO
  • Charge FrenzyEnrages and charges random targets every 2 seconds.
  • Spirit BondHeals 5% health per second, roughly 5,000 health per second.

How to handleThese mobs have enormous aggro range.

Ashtongue Mystic

KILL TARGET 1
  • Chain HealAround 16,000 initial healing and jumps to up to 3 nearby targets.
  • Flame ShockAround 3,500 initial damage plus 500 Fire every 3 seconds for 12 seconds.
  • Frost ShockAround 3,500 Frost and slows for 8 seconds.
  • BloodlustDispellable magic buff granting hostile mobs 35% increased attack and casting rate for 20 seconds.
  • Searing TotemDeals around 2,200 Fire every 2.2 seconds.
  • Windfury TotemBuffs nearby enemies with Windfury.

How to handleMystic is the priority kill target. Immediately swap to and kill Windfury Totem whenever summoned; keep Chain Heal pressure controlled.

Ashtongue Primalist

KILL TARGET 2
  • Wyvern StingSleeps a random target for 12 seconds, then applies a Nature DoT.
  • Multi-ShotNormal attack damage plus 3,400 Arcane to up to 3 targets.
  • Sweeping Wing ClipImmobilizes players within 5 yards for 10 seconds.

How to handlePrimalists follow Mystics in pack priority.

Ashtongue Stormcaller

CASTER
  • Chain LightningInitial hit around 4,500, with less damage on jumps.
  • Lightning BoltDeals 1,500–2,000 Nature.

Ashtongue Stalker

POISON CLEANSE
  • BlindPoison debuff that disables a random target for 10 seconds; poison cleansing removes it.
  • Mind-numbing PoisonSlows casting rate by 50%.
  • Instant PoisonAdds Nature damage to melee attacks.

How to handleCleanse Blind as a poison; this is static guidance, not a new roster assignment.
