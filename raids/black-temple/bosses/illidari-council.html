Pull · Separate All 4 · EVERY DPS ON GATHIOS
SEPARATE THE COUNCIL
Each assigned tank takes their Council member to the planned position. Do not allow the bosses to collapse together in the center.
LOCK YOUR ASSIGNMENT
Gathios, Zerevor, Malande, and Veras require different handling. Each tank/control player stays responsible for their assigned boss.
HEAL YOUR ASSIGNED CONTROL
Immediately lock onto your assigned tank or mage tank. The four Council members create simultaneous damage across the room.
EXPECT HEAVY RAID DAMAGE
Flamestrike, Blizzard, Deadly Poison, Envenom, and Malande's spells create constant raid damage. Maintain raid coverage while assigned healers stay locked on their control targets.
EVERY DPS ON GATHIOS
All four Council members share one health pool. Keep normal raid DPS focused on Gathios while the other three are controlled.
Do Not Split DPS
Do not independently attack Malande, Zerevor, or Veras unless performing a required mechanic. Damage on Gathios reduces the shared Council health.
Gathios · Main DPS Target · Move Out of Consecration
MOVE GATHIOS OUT OF CONSECRATION
Consecration lasts 20 seconds and deals 2,250 Holy damage every 3 seconds. Move Gathios and the melee group out immediately.
KEEP GATHIOS FAR FROM THE COUNCIL
Gathios alternates 30-second auras: Devotion Aura grants nearby Council members +20% Armor, while Chromatic Resistance Aura grants +250 resistance to all magic schools. Keep him well separated from the other three.
SPELL REFLECT JUDGEMENT OF COMMAND
A Warrior tank can Spell Reflect Judgement of Command to negate its 6,175–6,825 Holy damage spike.
WATCH GATHIOS TANK
Judgement of Blood deals 1,200 Holy damage every second for 9 seconds. Expect periodic tank pressure while moving with Gathios out of Consecration.
Move With the Tank
Do not remain standing in Consecration to finish a cast. Reposition and maintain healing coverage.
STAY ON GATHIOS
Gathios remains the normal raid damage target because the Council shares health. He can give other members 15 seconds of physical immunity with Blessing of Protection or magic immunity with Blessing of Spell Warding; do not switch for these blessings.
MOVE OUT OF CONSECRATION
Follow the boss when the tank relocates him. Never continue attacking while standing in Consecration.
Zerevor · MAGE TANK · Spellsteal Dampen Magic · Avoid AoE
DAMPEN MAGIC = SURVIVAL BUFF
Spellsteal Zerevor's Dampen Magic immediately. It reduces magic damage taken by 75% but also reduces healing received by 500. Do not let the buff fall off.
KEEP ZEREVOR STILL
Control Zerevor at range and keep him separated from the main raid. His 2-second Arcane Bolt hits for roughly 12,950 Arcane damage before mitigation, making stolen Dampen Magic essential.
STAY MORE THAN 10 YARDS AWAY
Anyone approaching within 10 yards can trigger Arcane Explosion for roughly 8,500 Arcane damage to nearby players. Maintain ranged control.
USE BIGGER HEALS ON MAGE TANK
Stay focused on the assigned Zerevor mage tank. Dampen Magic reduces healing received by a flat 500; favor larger, less frequent heals so the penalty does not punish many small heals.
Avoid Zerevor AoE
Flamestrike deals 4,625 initial damage in an 8-yard radius, then 2,806 every 2 seconds for 12 seconds. Blizzard deals 4,375 every 2 seconds while inside. Move immediately.
DO NOT APPROACH ZEREVOR
Zerevor is controlled by the mage tank. Normal DPS stays on Gathios and more than 10 yards from Zerevor.
MOVE FROM FLAMESTRIKE + BLIZZARD
Ground AoE from Zerevor must be avoided immediately. Do not finish casts while standing in it.
Malande · TWO INTERRUPT TYPES · Veras · Vanish ~60s · Stealth 30s
HOLD MALANDE SEPARATE
Malande remains controlled away from the main Gathios group while assigned kickers handle her dangerous casts.
PICK VERAS UP IMMEDIATELY
Veras Vanishes approximately every 60 seconds and remains out of normal combat for 30 seconds. Grab him immediately when he reappears before he attacks vulnerable raid members.
PALADIN / DRUID IDEAL FOR VERAS
Veras may reappear with physical or magic immunity from Gathios. A Paladin or Druid can use mixed damage types to secure the pickup more reliably. While he is vanished, assist with healing or raid utility without losing pickup readiness.
POISON → THEN ENVENOM
During stealth, Deadly Poison deals random players 1,000 Nature damage every second for 4 seconds. When it expires, Veras follows on the same player with a 4,250 Nature Envenom. Keep poisoned players high enough to survive the burst.
WATCH REFLECTIVE SHIELD DAMAGE
Malande's Reflective Shield absorbs 25,000 damage and reflects 50% of absorbed damage back to attackers. Be ready to heal players handling her interrupts.
PHYSICAL + MAGIC INTERRUPTS
Assign both interrupt types to Malande. Blessing of Protection can block physical attacks, while Blessing of Spell Warding blocks magic. Do not overlap interrupts and leave later casts uncovered.
KICK CIRCLE OF HEALING
Circle of Healing restores 95,000 health to all four Council members. It must be interrupted every time.
CIRCLE FIRST · INTERRUPT OTHER CASTS TOO
Circle of Healing is the highest priority, but interrupt Divine Wrath and Empowered Smite whenever coverage allows.
KEEP INTERRUPTING THROUGH SHIELD
Reflective Shield does not prevent interrupts. Continue covering Malande's casts even while the shield is active.
REFLECTIVE SHIELD = WATCH YOUR HEALTH
The shield absorbs 25,000 damage and reflects 50% of absorbed damage back to attackers. Normal DPS stays on Gathios; interrupters must watch their health.
Shared Health · Maintain Control · Burn Gathios Until Council Dies
DO NOT ABANDON YOUR BOSS
Shared health does not mean tanks should collapse all four bosses together. Maintain each control assignment through the entire encounter.
KEEP ALL 4 ASSIGNMENTS ALIVE
The encounter becomes unstable quickly if any tank or the Zerevor mage tank dies. Maintain assignment coverage while handling raid mechanics.
GATHIOS = SHARED HEALTH BURN
Continue burning Gathios. Every point of damage reduces the Council's shared health pool.
Mechanics Before Damage
Interrupt Malande and move from Zerevor AoE immediately, then return to Gathios.
Council Health Reaches 0 · All 4 Die Together
Maintain Separation
Maintain separation until the encounter ends.
Keep Every Assignment Alive
Keep every control assignment alive.
Finish the Shared Health Pool
Stay on Gathios and never miss Circle of Healing interrupts.

Council Rules

Gathios: DPS TARGET · KEEP FAR AWAY
Zerevor: MAGE TANK · SPELLSTEAL
Malande: PHYSICAL + MAGIC INTERRUPTS
Veras: SURVIVE POISON · FAST RE-PICKUP

Wipe Causes
  • Council members are not separated cleanly on the pull.
  • Gathios tank leaves the raid standing in Consecration.
  • Zerevor mage tank fails to Spellsteal Dampen Magic.
  • Player moves into Zerevor's 10-yard Arcane Explosion range.
  • Raid stands in Flamestrike or Blizzard.
  • Circle of Healing is not interrupted and Malande heals the Council.
  • Kickers overlap interrupts and leave the next Circle of Healing uncovered.
  • Zerevor mage tank dies and the mage boss enters the raid.
  • Deadly Poison target is not topped before the follow-up Envenom.
  • Tanks abandon their assignments because all four bosses share health.
  • DPS splits unnecessarily across Council members instead of maintaining the Gathios burn.
  • Gathios is near another Council member and buffs them with Devotion Aura or Chromatic Resistance Aura.
  • Gathios tank fails to Spell Reflect Judgement of Command when using the Warrior strategy.
  • Malande receives Blessing of Protection or Blessing of Spell Warding and the raid lacks the correct interrupt type.
  • Interrupters stop because Reflective Shield is active.
  • An attacker kills themselves through Reflective Shield's reflected damage.
  • Veras reappears after Vanish and is not picked up immediately.
