Pull · Separate All 4 · EVERY DPS ON GATHIOS
SEPARATE THE COUNCIL
Each assigned tank takes their Council member to the planned position. Do not allow the bosses to collapse together in the center.
LOCK YOUR ASSIGNMENT
Gathios, Zerevor, Malande, and Veras require different handling. Each tank/control player stays responsible for their assigned boss.
HEAL YOUR ASSIGNED CONTROL
Immediately lock onto your assigned tank or mage tank. The four Council members create simultaneous damage across the room.
Raid Healers Cover Mechanics
Raid healers cover Flamestrike, Blizzard, poison, and other unavoidable/random damage while assigned healers keep each control target alive.
EVERY DPS ON GATHIOS
All four Council members share one health pool. Keep normal raid DPS focused on Gathios while the other three are controlled.
Do Not Split DPS
Do not independently attack Malande, Zerevor, or Veras unless performing a required mechanic. Damage on Gathios reduces the shared Council health.
Gathios · Main DPS Target · Move Out of Consecration
MOVE GATHIOS OUT OF CONSECRATION
When Gathios drops Consecration, move him out immediately and re-establish stable positioning.
Keep Gathios Away From Zerevor
Maintain separation from the mage-tank area and keep the primary DPS group clear of Zerevor's dangerous AoE abilities.
WATCH GATHIOS TANK
Gathios is the active melee boss and primary DPS target. Keep his tank stable while moving with the boss out of Consecration.
Move With the Tank
Do not remain standing in Consecration to finish a cast. Reposition and maintain healing coverage.
STAY ON GATHIOS
Gathios remains the normal raid damage target because the Council shares health.
MOVE OUT OF CONSECRATION
Follow the boss when the tank relocates him. Never continue attacking while standing in Consecration.
Zerevor · MAGE TANK · Spellsteal Dampen Magic · Avoid AoE
MAGE TANK SPELLSTEALS DAMPEN MAGIC
The assigned mage tank Spellsteals Zerevor's Dampen Magic and uses it to survive while maintaining ranged threat.
KEEP ZEREVOR STILL
Control Zerevor at range and keep him separated from the main raid. Do not drag him through the DPS group.
NEVER STAND CLOSE
Zerevor's Arcane Explosion punishes players who approach him. Maintain ranged control.
KEEP MAGE TANK ALIVE
The Zerevor healer assignment stays focused on the mage tank while they maintain Spellsteal and ranged threat.
Avoid Zerevor AoE
Do not stand in Flamestrike or Blizzard while healing the mage tank or raid.
DO NOT APPROACH ZEREVOR
Zerevor is controlled by the mage tank. Normal DPS stays on Gathios and out of Zerevor's Arcane Explosion range.
MOVE FROM FLAMESTRIKE + BLIZZARD
Ground AoE from Zerevor must be avoided immediately. Do not finish casts while standing in it.
Malande · INTERRUPT HEALS · Veras · Survive Poison
HOLD MALANDE SEPARATE
Malande remains controlled away from the main Gathios group while assigned kickers handle her dangerous casts.
CONTROL VERAS WHEN VISIBLE
Veras periodically vanishes. When he is visible, the assigned Veras tank/control player keeps him managed and away from vulnerable players.
WATCH DEADLY POISON TARGETS
Veras applies dangerous Deadly Poison to raid members while moving through his vanish cycle. Be ready for sudden damage on random players.
Malande May Become Immune
Divine Shield periodically makes Malande immune. Do not waste raid attention trying to force damage through the immunity.
KICK CIRCLE OF HEALING
Assigned interrupters must stop Malande's Circle of Healing. Allowing the heal through restores Council health and extends the encounter.
KEEP KICK ROTATION CLEAN
Do not overlap every interrupt. Preserve enough coverage to stop repeated Circle of Healing casts.
NORMAL DPS STAYS ON GATHIOS
Only players handling required interrupts/control need to interact with Malande. Everyone else remains on Gathios.
Shared Health · Maintain Control · Burn Gathios Until Council Dies
DO NOT ABANDON YOUR BOSS
Shared health does not mean tanks should collapse all four bosses together. Maintain each control assignment through the entire encounter.
KEEP ALL 4 ASSIGNMENTS ALIVE
The encounter becomes unstable quickly if any tank or the Zerevor mage tank dies. Maintain assignment coverage while handling raid mechanics.
GATHIOS = SHARED HEALTH BURN
Continue burning Gathios. Every point of damage reduces the Council's shared health pool.
Mechanics Before Damage
Interrupt Malande and move from Zerevor AoE immediately, then return to Gathios.
Council Health Reaches 0 · All 4 Die Together
Maintain Separation
Maintain separation until the encounter ends.
Keep Every Assignment Alive
Keep every control assignment alive.
Finish the Shared Health Pool
Stay on Gathios and never miss Circle of Healing interrupts.

Council Rules

Gathios: DPS TARGET
Zerevor: MAGE TANK
Malande: KICK HEAL
Veras: SURVIVE POISON

Wipe Causes
  • Council members are not separated cleanly on the pull.
  • Gathios tank leaves the raid standing in Consecration.
  • Zerevor mage tank fails to Spellsteal Dampen Magic.
  • Player moves into Zerevor's Arcane Explosion range.
  • Raid stands in Flamestrike or Blizzard.
  • Circle of Healing is not interrupted and Malande heals the Council.
  • Kickers overlap interrupts and leave the next Circle of Healing uncovered.
  • Zerevor mage tank dies and the mage boss enters the raid.
  • Veras poison damage is not healed.
  • Tanks abandon their assignments because all four bosses share health.
  • DPS splits unnecessarily across Council members instead of maintaining the Gathios burn.
  • Gathios and Zerevor are positioned too close together and their mechanics overlap the raid.
