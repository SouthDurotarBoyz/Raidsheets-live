Pre-Pull · Spread Out · Survive Needle Spine
Tank Naj'entus Still
Main tank holds Naj'entus in place and keeps him stable for the entire fight. No tank swap is required.
Raid Spread
Do not drag the boss through the raid. Give melee room to spread around the boss.
Keep Everyone Healthy
Needle Spine hits players constantly. Keep the raid healthy enough to survive a follow-up Impaling Spine or Tidal Burst.
Spread 6+ Yards
Needle Spine splashes players near its target. Healers spread around the room while remaining in range of the raid.
Spread 6+ Yards
Spread around Naj'entus so Needle Spine does not splash multiple raiders. Melee spread around the back and sides.
Full Damage
Stay on Naj'entus whenever Tidal Shield is not active.
Impaling Spine · Free the Target Immediately
Main Tank Cannot Be Impaled
Impaling Spine targets other raiders, so keep Naj'entus stable while the raid handles the Spine.
Heal the Impaled Player
Impaling Spine hits hard, stuns the target, and continues dealing damage until another player removes the Spine. Heal the target immediately.
Keep Moving Players Alive
A nearby raider must move to the impaled player and click the Spine. Cover both the target and raid damage while the Spine is removed.
Click the Spine
If a nearby player is impaled, immediately move to them and click the Spine sticking out of the player. This frees them and gives you a Naj'entus Spine.
Keep the Spine
The player who removes the Spine receives a Naj'entus Spine. Save it for the next Tidal Shield.
Tidal Shield · TOP RAID · Throw Spine
Shield = Stop Damage
Naj'entus becomes immune while Tidal Shield is active and heals while the shield remains. Hold the boss steady.
Prepare for Tidal Burst
The main tank also takes the raid-wide burst when the shield breaks. Use a defensive if needed and make sure you are healthy first.
TOP THE RAID FIRST
Do not break Tidal Shield until the entire raid is healed. Breaking it causes roughly 8,500 Frost damage to everyone.
Heal Immediately After Burst
Tidal Burst drops the entire raid at once. Begin raid healing immediately after the shield breaks.
DO NOT THROW EARLY
If you have a Naj'entus Spine, wait for the raid lead or healer call before throwing it. Breaking the shield too early can kill the raid.
Throw Spine at Naj'entus
Once the raid is topped, a player holding a Naj'entus Spine throws it at the boss to break Tidal Shield. Resume full damage immediately.
Repeat Until Dead · Beat the 8-Minute Berserk
Hold Naj'entus Still
Recover the Raid
Top everyone before the next Needle Spine / Impaling Spine sequence.
Repeat the Cycle
Collect Spines and break shields until Naj'entus dies.
Naj'entus Positioning
Raid positioning for the Naj'entus encounter.
Wipe Causes
  • Tidal Shield is broken before the raid is fully healed.
  • Impaling Spine is not removed quickly and the impaled player dies.
  • Players stack together and Needle Spine splashes multiple raiders.
  • Raid enters a Tidal Burst with players already damaged from Needle Spine.
  • No player saves a Naj'entus Spine for the next Tidal Shield.
  • Player with the Spine is too far away or fails to throw it while Naj'entus heals behind Tidal Shield.
  • Healers do not immediately recover the raid after Tidal Burst.
  • Raid damage is too low and Naj'entus reaches Berserk.
