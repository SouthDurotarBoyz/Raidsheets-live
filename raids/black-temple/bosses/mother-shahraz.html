ALL 3 TANKS STACK IN FRONT
Tank 1, Tank 2, and Tank 3 stand together directly in front of Mother Shahraz so Saber Lash is split between all three.
DO NOT SEPARATE THE TANKS
Saber Lash can kill a tank if its damage is not properly shared. Maintain the three-player tank stack throughout the fight.
SABER LASH PROTECTS TANKS
Saber Lash not only splits its massive physical damage among the three tanks; players hit by it are also immune to Mother's beam abilities and Fatal Attraction. Tanks therefore do not need Shadow Resistance.
Hold Mother Still
Keep Mother facing the tank group and away from the raid. Stable positioning makes every other mechanic easier.
STAY MORE THAN 18 YARDS AWAY
Silencing Shriek silences players near Mother for 10 seconds. Healers remain at range so the healing team is not simultaneously shut down.
Cover All 3 Tanks
Saber Lash repeatedly hits the tank group. Maintain the assigned tank healing while raid healers cover beam and Fatal Attraction damage.
USE WALLS / FISH STATUE
Ranged and healers can position against walls or beneath the decorative fish fountain/statue so Sinister Beam knockbacks do not throw them across the room.
ONLY TANKS IN FRONT
Melee attacks from behind Mother. Never join the three tanks in front or interfere with Saber Lash positioning.
Casters Stay More Than 18y
Ranged casters remain outside Silencing Shriek range so they are not locked out for 10 seconds.
Full Damage
Maintain damage while preserving the encounter's strict tank and ranged positioning.
Fatal Attraction · 3 Players Teleported · RUN APART NOW
DO NOT MOVE MOTHER
Fatal Attraction targets non-tanks. Keep Mother stable while the affected players handle the mechanic away from the boss.
Keep Saber Lash Stack
Do not break the three-tank formation because Fatal Attraction occurred elsewhere in the room.
FATAL ATTRACTION = HEAL FAST
Three players are teleported together and take Shadow damage every second while linked. They are the highest raid-healing priority until their links break; immediately stabilize them while they separate.
DO NOT CHASE INTO THE STACK
Heal affected players from safe range when possible. The solution is for the three linked players to separate, not for more players to gather around them.
IF LINKED — RUN AWAY FROM EACH OTHER
Fatal Attraction teleports three players together. Immediately choose different directions and create distance from BOTH other linked players.
GET 25+ YARDS APART
BREAK THE PURPLE BEAMS: run in different directions until you are at least 25 yards away from both other linked players.
Return After Links Break
Once Fatal Attraction ends, return to normal raid positioning and resume damage.
Rocket Boots Help Fatal Attraction
Rocket Boots Xtreme or Rocket Boots Xtreme Lite can help a linked player create 25+ yards of separation quickly; they are optional, not required.
Shadow Beams · Raid Damage + Knockback + DoT + Mana Burn
Tanks Are Protected
Saber Lash protects its targets from Mother's Shadow beams and Fatal Attraction. Continue holding the boss; tanks do not need Shadow Resistance.
EXPECT CONSTANT RAID DAMAGE
Mother fires several Shadow beam effects into the raid. Be ready for direct damage, damage-over-time effects, knockbacks, and mana loss.
BEAMS CAN CHAIN TO 10 PLAYERS
Mother's beam attacks target a random player and can jump to nearby players, affecting up to 10 raiders.
Know Every Beam
Sinful Beam → 6,938 Shadow damage. Sinister Beam → 2,000 Shadow damage plus knockback. Vile Beam → 2,500 Shadow damage every 2s for 8s. Wicked Beam burns 1,000 mana.
Prioritize Fatal Attraction Damage
Normal beam damage is frequent, but Fatal Attraction players can lose health extremely quickly while still linked.
NON-TANKS: ~174 SR + PRAYER RECOMMENDED
A traditional safer setup uses roughly 174 Shadow Resistance from gear plus 70 from Prayer of Shadow Protection. This is recommended, not an absolute gear gate: raids can win with little or no Shadow Resistance, while higher resistance provides more margin for error.
Recover From Knockbacks
Sinister Beam can knock players backward. Return to safe positioning without moving into the tank side or within Silencing Shriek range.
Manage Your Mana
Wicked Beam burns 1,000 mana. Mana users should preserve and use normal recovery tools as needed.
Silencing Shriek + Prismatic Aura · Stay at Range · Adapt Damage
Keep Mother Facing Away
Maintain the same three-tank position. The ranged mechanics do not change the tank formation.
18 YARDS MEANS SILENCE
Do not drift toward Mother. A healer caught by Silencing Shriek can be unable to cast for 10 seconds during heavy raid damage.
Maintain Raid Coverage
Keep ranged and Fatal Attraction players in healing range without moving into Mother's silence radius.
PRISMATIC AURA CHANGES DAMAGE SCHOOLS
Prismatic Aura cycles among Shadow, Arcane, Fire, Nature, Frost, and Holy effects, reducing one school and amplifying others. Avoid a heavily reduced school and favor an amplified school when your class can switch.
Do Not Sacrifice Positioning for DPS
Fatal Attraction, Silencing Shriek, and safe beam positioning take priority over squeezing in one extra cast.
Repeat · Break Every Fatal Attraction · Kill Mother Shahraz
Stay stacked and split every Saber Lash.
Remain more than 18y away and keep Fatal Attraction targets alive.
Run apart instantly if linked, then return and burn.

  1. 3 non-tanks teleported.
  2. RUN IN DIFFERENT DIRECTIONS.
  3. GET 25+ YARDS APART.
  4. Break all links.
  5. Return to raid.
Wipe Causes
  • One of the three Saber Lash tanks is out of position and the remaining tanks take too much damage.
  • A non-tank stands in front of Mother and interferes with Saber Lash positioning.
  • Mother is turned toward the raid.
  • Fatal Attraction players remain together after being teleported.
  • Two Fatal Attraction players run in the same direction and fail to break their link.
  • Fatal Attraction players fail to get at least 25 yards apart.
  • Healers stand within 18 yards and are silenced by Silencing Shriek.
  • Ranged players drift too close to Mother during movement.
  • Fatal Attraction targets are not healed while attempting to separate.
  • Sinister Beam knockbacks send players into unsafe positions.
  • Raid takes excessive beam damage from low survivability or Shadow Resistance while using the safer SR strategy.
  • Players fail to adapt to Prismatic Aura when they have a viable alternate damage school.
