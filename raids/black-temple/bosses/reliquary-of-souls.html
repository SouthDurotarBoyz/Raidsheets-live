Essence of Suffering · Fixate Every 5s · Closest Player Controls Boss · NO HEALING
CLOSEST PLAYER = FIXATE
Every 5 seconds, Essence of Suffering chooses the closest player as its new Fixate target. There is no normal threat table in this phase.
ROTATE TANKS
Tank 1 starts closest. When the active tank needs relief, Tank 2 moves closer and takes the next Fixate while the previous tank backs away.
HIGH AVOIDANCE MATTERS
With healing disabled, survival depends on avoidance, mitigation, defensive cooldowns, and rotating Fixate cleanly.
ENRAGE ~45s · 15s
Enrage occurs roughly 45 seconds into the phase and lasts 15 seconds. During Enrage, use a high-avoidance Fixate target such as a Rogue with Evasion or Hunter with Deterrence. Otherwise use a tank defensive.
NO HEALING OR HEALTH REGEN
Aura of Suffering reduces healing and health regeneration by 100%, removes Armor, and reduces Defense skill by 500.
HEALERS DPS
With healing disabled, contribute damage to Essence of Suffering. Do not conserve mana; the intermission restores health and mana.
DISPEL SOUL DRAIN
Soul Drain targets random players, deals damage, and drains mana. It is a Magic debuff and should be removed immediately.
NO THREAT TABLE
You cannot pull threat during Essence of Suffering. Stay behind the assigned Fixate player and maximize damage.
NO ARMOR DEBUFFS NEEDED
Essence of Suffering has 0 Armor. Expose Armor, Curse of Recklessness, and non-Improved Faerie Fire provide no armor-reduction benefit here.
DO NOT STEAL FIXATE
Never move closer to the boss than the assigned Fixate player.
Enslaved Souls · Kill Weak Ghosts · Recover Health and Mana
GATHER THE SOULS
Control the weak Enslaved Souls so the raid can kill them efficiently.
KILL SOULS = RAID RECOVERS
Each Enslaved Soul killed restores health and mana to the entire raid. Finish the intermission recovered before Essence of Desire.
KILL THE SOULS
AoE the weak ghosts quickly; every death restores health and mana to the entire raid.
Essence of Desire · 50% DAMAGE REFLECT · Spirit Shock Every 5s · Remove Rune Shield
SPELL REFLECT DEADEN
Deaden doubles damage taken for 10 seconds. A Warrior Main Tank should Spell Reflect it when possible, putting the effect on Essence of Desire instead.
HOLD DESIRE STABLE
Maintain control while the raid handles every Spirit Shock and reflected damage.
HEALING DOUBLED · MAX MANA SHRINKS
Aura of Desire doubles healing output but gradually reduces maximum mana. Spend mana carefully; the next intermission restores it.
DISPEL / SPELLSTEAL RUNE SHIELD
Rune Shield absorbs damage and prevents Spirit Shock from being interrupted. Remove it immediately with Dispel Magic or Spellsteal.
WATCH HIGH-DAMAGE PLAYERS
Keep attackers healthy enough to survive the damage reflected by their next attack.
50% OF YOUR DAMAGE HITS YOU
Damage dealers take self-damage equal to 50% of the damage they deal to Essence of Desire. Avoid lethal burst spikes.
SPIRIT SHOCK EVERY ~5s
Spirit Shock deals massive damage and disorients the Main Tank. Every cast must be interrupted. Use a clean two-player kick rotation so every Spirit Shock is covered; Curse of Tongues makes interrupts safer.
REFLECTED DEADEN = HUGE BURN
When Deaden is reflected onto the boss, Essence takes double damage for 10 seconds—but Aura of Desire also means DPS takes much more reflected self-damage. Do not kill yourself.
Enslaved Souls · Kill Weak Ghosts · Enter Phase 3 Full
GATHER THE SOULS
Control the weak ghosts for a fast recovery.
ENTER PHASE 3 FULL
Every Soul kill restores raid health and mana. Do not begin Essence of Anger depleted.
AOE SOULS
Kill every Soul quickly and prepare offensive cooldowns.
Essence of Anger · ONE ACTIVE TANK · Seethe Threat · Soul Scream · FINAL BURN
ONE ACTIVE TANK
Avoid unnecessary Taunts during Essence of Anger. Use one active tank unless deliberately executing the planned Seethe opener.
TAUNT = SEETHE
Any Taunt gives everyone except the taunter +200% threat generation for 10 seconds and gives Essence of Anger +100% attack speed for 10 seconds.
OPTIONAL OPENER: OT TAUNT ONCE
An off-tank can Taunt once at the start so the Main Tank gains Seethe and builds a large threat lead for roughly 5–10 seconds. The raid waits for the 10-second threat buff to fade before full DPS; do not repeat the swap.
FACE ANGER AWAY
Soul Scream is a frontal cone. Keep Essence of Anger facing away from the raid.
LOW RESOURCE FOR SOUL SCREAM
Soul Scream is a frontal that burns mana or rage. Keep your resource low before it lands to reduce the resulting damage.
DAMAGE RAMPS BOTH WAYS
Aura of Anger steadily increases raid-wide Shadow damage while also increasing the raid's damage output. This becomes a race to finish the Essence.
SPITE · IMMUNE 6s → ~7,444 NATURE
Spite makes the target immune to damage for 6 seconds, then hits them for about 7,444 Nature damage. Top them before the immunity ends.
NATURE PROTECTION HELPS
A Major Nature Protection Potion can help absorb the Spite spike.
TRANQ BOSS SEETHE
Hunters should use Tranquilizing Shot on the boss's +100% attack-speed Seethe buff after a Taunt.
BLOODLUST + ALL COOLDOWNS
Aura of Anger steadily ramps incoming damage, so this is the final DPS race. Use Bloodlust/Heroism and offensive cooldowns as the raid's strategy.
STAY OUT OF FRONT
Only the tank belongs in front. Do not take Soul Scream with the tank.
WATCH SEETHE THREAT
Do not pull threat while affected by +200% threat generation. Wait out the planned opener before full damage.
Essence of Anger Dies · Encounter Over
KEEP ANGER CONTROLLED
Maintain control through the final burn.
DO NOT CONSERVE COOLDOWNS
DO NOT STOP
Keep attacking until the Essence dies.
Reliquary of Souls Positioning
Raid positioning for the Reliquary of Souls encounter.
Three Rules

Suffering: Closest player controls Fixate every 5s. No healing.

Desire: Kick every Spirit Shock. Spell Reflect Deaden. Remove Rune Shield.

Anger: Avoid unnecessary Taunts. Watch Seethe, Soul Scream, and Spite.

Wipe Causes
  • The Fixate rotation fails or a player stands closer than the assigned target during Essence of Suffering.
  • Soul Drain is not dispelled immediately.
  • The raid wastes Phase 1 trying to heal instead of dealing damage and surviving.
  • Enrage begins without an avoidance or defensive Fixate plan.
  • The raid fails to recover health and mana from Enslaved Souls.
  • Spirit Shock is not interrupted.
  • Rune Shield remains active and makes Spirit Shock uninterruptible.
  • Deaden is mishandled or the Warrior misses a planned Spell Reflect.
  • DPS kills themselves through Aura of Desire's 50% reflection.
  • An unnecessary Taunt triggers dangerous Seethe threat.
  • The boss's Seethe attack-speed buff is not removed with Tranquilizing Shot.
  • DPS pulls threat while affected by +200% Seethe.
  • Soul Scream hits the raid or the tank carries too much rage or mana into it.
  • A Spite target is not topped before the delayed Nature hit.
  • Aura of Anger outscales healing before the boss dies.
