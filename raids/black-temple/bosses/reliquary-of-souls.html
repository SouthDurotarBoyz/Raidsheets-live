Essence of Suffering · NO NORMAL HEALING · Rotate Fixate
CLOSEST PLAYER GETS FIXATE
Essence of Suffering repeatedly Fixates the closest player. Tanks control the mechanic by deliberately taking turns being closest to the boss.
ROTATE TANKS
Tank 1 starts closest. When the active tank needs relief, Tank 2 moves closer and takes the next Fixate while the previous tank backs away.
Use Personal Survival
Normal healing is unavailable during this phase. Use mitigation and personal survival tools while controlling Fixate.
YOU CANNOT HEAL NORMALLY
Aura of Suffering prevents normal healing. Do not waste the phase repeatedly attempting conventional heals that cannot restore the raid.
SURVIVAL TOOLS MATTER
Use shields, absorbs, Healthstones, potions, mitigation, and any applicable nonstandard survival tools. Keep yourself alive for later phases.
Prepare for Phase 2
Preserve positioning and be ready to immediately resume active healing once Essence of Suffering dies.
BURN SUFFERING
Essence of Suffering has extremely weak defenses. Deal maximum damage while staying behind the tanks and never becoming the closest player.
TRANQ ENRAGE
Hunters remove Enrage immediately with Tranquilizing Shot. Do not allow an enraged Essence to remain on the active Fixate tank.
Do Not Steal Fixate
Never move closer to the boss than the active tank. The closest player can become the Fixate target.
Manage Soul Drain
Soul Drain damages players and drains mana. Continue the burn while using personal survival tools as needed.
Enslaved Souls · AoE Them Down · Recover Resources
Gather the Souls
Control the Enslaved Souls so the raid can kill them efficiently.
RECOVER
Healing is available again. Stabilize the raid and use the intermission to prepare for Essence of Desire.
KILL THE SOULS
AoE the Enslaved Souls quickly. Their deaths help restore raid resources before the next Essence.
Essence of Desire · REFLECTED DAMAGE · Interrupt Everything
Hold Desire Stable
Keep Essence of Desire facing predictably and maintain control while the raid manages interrupts and reflected damage.
Watch Your Own Damage
Tanks are also affected by the phase's unusual damage mechanics. Do not ignore your own health while maintaining threat.
HEAL REFLECTED DAMAGE
Aura of Desire reflects a portion of player damage back to the attacker. Expect constant raid-wide self-inflicted damage as DPS attacks.
Watch High-Damage Players
Players dealing large bursts can take large reflected hits. Keep them healthy enough that their next attack does not kill them.
Mana Behaves Differently
Use the phase's unusual mana environment aggressively while maintaining enough control to enter Phase 3 prepared.
YOUR DAMAGE HURTS YOU
Aura of Desire reflects damage back to you. Watch your health and stop attacking briefly if another hit would kill you.
INTERRUPT SPIRIT SHOCK
Assigned kickers must stop Spirit Shock. Keep the interrupt rotation clean and do not overlap every kick.
INTERRUPT DEADEN
Do not allow Deaden to remain uncontrolled. Assigned interrupt players should handle it immediately according to the raid's kick rotation.
REMOVE RUNE SHIELD
Rune Shield protects the Essence. The assigned Magic Dispel player removes the shield quickly so normal damage can resume.
Enslaved Souls · Kill Fast · Prepare for Final Burn
Gather the Souls
Top the Raid
Prepare for escalating Phase 3 damage.
AoE Souls
Kill them quickly and prepare offensive cooldowns.
Essence of Anger · BLOODLUST · Kill Before Raid Collapses
FACE ANGER AWAY
Keep Essence of Anger facing away from the raid. Soul Scream is a dangerous frontal mechanic and should only threaten the tank.
SURVIVE ESCALATING DAMAGE
Aura of Anger continuously makes the phase more dangerous. Use defensive cooldowns as incoming damage ramps.
DAMAGE ONLY GETS WORSE
Aura of Anger continuously increases the encounter's damage pressure. Heal aggressively; this phase is a race, not a conservation phase.
WATCH SPITE TARGETS
Players affected by Spite enter a dangerous delayed-damage window. Keep them healthy and be ready for the effect to resolve.
Use Healing Cooldowns
Use major raid and tank healing cooldowns during the final burn rather than saving them.
BLOODLUST + ALL COOLDOWNS
Use Bloodlust/Heroism and offensive cooldowns on Essence of Anger. Kill it before the escalating aura overwhelms the raid.
STAY OUT OF FRONT
Only the tank belongs in front of Essence of Anger. Do not take Soul Scream with the tank.
Burn Through the Ramp
Damage pressure continually increases. This is the final phase; maximize damage while handling Spite correctly.

Keep Anger Controlled
Maintain control through the final burn.

Three Rules

Suffering: Closest player controls Fixate. No normal healing.

Desire: Interrupt. Watch reflected damage.

Anger: Bloodlust. Burn.

Wipe Causes
  • A non-tank stands closest to Essence of Suffering and receives Fixate.
  • Tanks fail to rotate Fixate and the active tank dies during the no-healing phase.
  • Hunter fails to remove Enrage from Essence of Suffering.
  • Players waste Phase 1 trying to use normal healing instead of survival tools.
  • Raid fails to kill Enslaved Souls efficiently and enters the next phase without recovering.
  • Spirit Shock casts go through during Essence of Desire.
  • Interrupt rotation fails on Deaden.
  • Rune Shield remains active too long.
  • DPS kills themselves by attacking through low health during reflected-damage Phase 2.
  • Raid enters Essence of Anger without recovering during the intermission.
  • Essence of Anger faces the raid and Soul Scream hits non-tanks.
  • Spite targets are not prepared for the delayed damage.
  • Raid holds Bloodlust or offensive cooldowns too long in the final phase.
  • Aura of Anger ramps until healers can no longer keep the raid alive.
