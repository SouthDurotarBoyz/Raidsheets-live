Phase 1 · Illidan 100% → 65% · Spread · Avoid Shear
KEEP SHEAR FROM LANDING
Illidan's Shear can catastrophically reduce the tank's maximum health. Maintain the encounter's required avoidance/defensive handling so Shear does not land normally.
FACE ILLIDAN AWAY
Keep Illidan facing away from the raid and hold him in a stable position.
Move From Flame Crash
Illidan creates dangerous fire on the ground. Reposition him enough to keep the tank and melee out without dragging him across the room.
WATCH THE ILLIDAN TANK
Tank damage is heavy and becomes lethal if Shear is mishandled. Keep the assigned Illidan tank stable.
Spread for Raid Mechanics
Maintain enough spacing that targeted effects do not unnecessarily hit multiple players.
STAY OUT OF FRONT
Only the Illidan tank belongs in front of the boss.
MOVE FROM FIRE
Do not remain in Flame Crash or other fire effects to finish attacks.
Burn to 65%
Push Illidan to the Phase 2 transition while preserving clean positioning.
Phase 2 · Flames of Azzinoth · TWO FLAME TANKS · Stay Near Your Glaive
ONE TANK PER FLAME
Left Flame tank controls one Flame of Azzinoth. Right Flame tank controls the other.
KEEP EACH FLAME NEAR ITS GLAIVE
Do not drag a Flame too far from the Warglaive that spawned it. Excessive distance causes the Flame to become dramatically more dangerous.
DO NOT LET THE FLAMES MEET
Keep the two Flames separated and under control throughout Phase 2.
LOCK ON YOUR FLAME TANK
Each Flame tank takes heavy Fire damage. Maintain dedicated healing on the assigned Left or Right Flame tank.
WATCH FOR EYE BEAM
Illidan's Eye Blast sweeps across the platform and leaves dangerous fire. Move immediately if the beam path threatens your position.
KILL THE FLAMES
Split as directed and kill both Flames of Azzinoth while respecting their tank positions.
DO NOT CROSS THE BEAM
Eye Blast and its fire trail are lethal. Reposition around the platform rather than crossing dangerous ground.
Stay With Your Side
Do not drag yourself or mechanics across the opposite Flame tank's area.
Both Flames Die · Illidan Lands · Main Tank Picks Him Up
Illidan tank immediately re-establishes control.
Transition healing from Flame tanks back to Illidan tank and raid.
Let the main tank establish Illidan before full damage resumes.
Phase 3 · Illidan 65% → 30% · Parasites · Normal Form
RE-ESTABLISH ILLIDAN
Main tank controls Illidan after landing and resumes normal positioning.
Continue Shear Handling
The dangerous tank mechanic remains relevant. Do not relax defensive/avoidance handling after Phase 2.
WATCH PARASITE TARGETS
Parasitic Shadowfiend creates additional enemies if handled poorly. Keep affected players alive while they move to the designated handling area.
Prepare for Demon Form
Normal-form healing changes sharply when Illidan transforms. Keep the raid stable before each transition.
PARASITE = MOVE OUT
If targeted by Parasitic Shadowfiend, move to the designated parasite area away from the main raid before additional Shadowfiends spawn.
KILL PARASITES FAST
Immediately kill spawned Parasitic Shadowfiends before they spread the mechanic to additional players.
Burn Illidan
Continue boss damage between mechanics and prepare to spread for Demon Form.
Demon Form · SPREAD OUT · Shadow Demons = KILL IMMEDIATELY
DEMON FORM CHANGES THE FIGHT
Illidan becomes a ranged Shadow-damage threat during Demon Form. Maintain the planned Demon Phase positioning rather than treating this like normal melee tanking.
Prepare for Normal Form Return
Be ready to immediately regain standard Illidan control when Demon Form ends.
SPREAD OUT
Demon Form creates dangerous ranged Shadow damage. Maintain separation and be ready for heavy raid damage.
SHADOW DEMONS = EMERGENCY
Shadow Demons target raid members and can quickly cause deaths if they reach their targets. Keep targeted players alive while DPS destroys the demons.
SHADOW DEMONS FIRST
When Shadow Demons spawn, immediately stop attacking Illidan and kill the demons.
KILL YOUR DEMON BEFORE IT REACHES YOU
Shadow Demons pursue targeted players. Every second matters; switch instantly and burn them down.
SPREAD FOR DEMON FORM
Do not stack the raid during Demon Form. Maintain safe separation while continuing damage when no Shadow Demons are active.
Normal Form ↔ Demon Form · Repeat Until 30%
Regain Illidan whenever normal form returns.
Shift between tank healing and spread raid healing.
Boss in normal form; Shadow Demons take absolute priority in Demon Form.
Phase 5 · Illidan 30% → 0% · Maiev · TRAP ILLIDAN · Final Burn
DRAG ILLIDAN INTO MAIEV'S TRAP
When Maiev places a trap, position Illidan over it so the trap activates and controls him.
KEEP CONTROL BETWEEN TRAPS
Resume normal Illidan tanking whenever he is not trapped. Continue respecting dangerous tank mechanics.
FINAL PHASE DAMAGE
Keep the Illidan tank and raid alive through the final burn. Do not conserve major healing cooldowns unnecessarily.
Stay Clear of Trap Positioning
Give the tank room to move Illidan into Maiev's trap without blocking the route or standing in dangerous ground.
TRAP ACTIVATES → BURN
When Illidan is trapped and vulnerable, maximize damage.
BLOODLUST + COOLDOWNS
Use Bloodlust/Heroism and remaining offensive cooldowns for the final burn according to raid timing.
Mechanics Still Matter
Do not die to fire, Parasites, or other mechanics simply because Illidan is below 30%.
Illidan Dies · Black Temple Clear
Keep Illidan controlled through the final burn.
Use remaining cooldowns.
Finish Illidan.

Wipe Causes
  • Shear lands normally and the Illidan tank becomes unhealable.
  • Illidan faces the raid.
  • Tank or melee remain in Flame Crash.
  • A Flame of Azzinoth is dragged too far from its Warglaive.
  • The two Flame tanks lose control of their sides.
  • Players cross Eye Blast or its fire trail.
  • Flame tank dies during Phase 2.
  • Illidan lands and the main tank does not immediately regain control.
  • Parasitic Shadowfiend target remains inside the raid.
  • Spawned Parasites are not killed quickly and spread to more players.
  • Raid stacks too tightly during Demon Form.
  • DPS fails to immediately switch to Shadow Demons.
  • Shadow Demon reaches its target.
  • Tank is not prepared when Illidan returns to normal form.
  • Illidan is not moved onto Maiev's trap in the final phase.
  • Raid holds major cooldowns too long and loses the final damage race.
