Phase 1 · 100% → 65% · Shear · Flame Crash · Draw Soul · Parasites
NEVER EAT SHEAR
Shear reduces maximum health by 60% for 7 seconds if it lands. The Illidan tank must maintain enough block/dodge/parry coverage that Shear is blocked, dodged, or parried rather than applied.
FACE ILLIDAN AWAY — DRAW SOUL
Draw Soul hits everyone in front for 4,500 Shadow damage and heals Illidan for 100,000 health per player hit. Only the tank belongs in front.
FLAME CRASH = REPOSITION
After Flame Crash, move Illidan slightly out of the blue fire. The persistent flame deals 5,000 Fire damage every 2 seconds.
WATCH THE ILLIDAN TANK
Keep the physical-mitigation tank stable; a landed Shear is immediately lethal territory.
WATCH PARASITE TARGETS
Parasitic Shadowfiend deals 3,000 Shadow damage every 2 seconds for 10 seconds. Heal the target while they reach the designated area.
STAY BEHIND ILLIDAN
Avoid Draw Soul's frontal cone so Illidan does not heal from extra targets.
KEEP HEALING REDUCTION ACTIVE
Maintain one healing-reduction effect such as Wound Poison, Mortal Strike, or Aimed Shot to reduce Draw Soul healing. These effects do not stack.
PARASITE = MOVE TO DESIGNATED AREA
Parasitic Shadowfiend deals 3,000 Shadow every 2 seconds for 10 seconds. Move before it expires; two Shadowfiends spawn at your feet.
RANGED KILL PARASITES
Immediately kill both spawned Shadowfiends before they reach and infect another player. Hunter Frost Trap at the parasite spot can immediately slow them.
Phase 2 · Flames of Azzinoth · 2 FR Tanks · Center Groups 11y+ Apart
ONE FR TANK PER FLAME
Illidan is airborne and is not tanked. Each Fire Resistance-capped tank controls one Flame.
DO NOT BREAK THE GLAIVE TETHER
Keep each Flame near its own Warglaive. Moving too far triggers Uncaged Wrath: +500% damage and +200% movement speed.
FACE FLAME AWAY
Each Flame uses Flame Blast in a frontal cone. Keep it faced away from the raid and melee.
DRAG FLAME SLOWLY
Move slowly around its Warglaive to avoid standing in Blaze, the green fire left behind, while keeping the tether intact.
LOCK ON YOUR FLAME TANK
Maintain dedicated healing on the assigned Left or Right Flame tank.
DARK BARRAGE = FOCUS HEAL
Dark Barrage deals 3,000 Shadow damage every second for 10 seconds. Immediately focus healing on the target.
3–4 GROUPS · 11+ YARDS APART
Form tight groups inside the grated center circle, each at least 11 yards from the others to limit Fireball splash.
EYE BEAMS = DO NOT CROSS
Illidan fires two beams with massive impact damage; their persistent blue fire deals about 5,000 Fire damage per second.
STAY IN THE CENTER CIRCLE
Ranged and healers stay inside the grated area between the Warglaives. Moving too far away can cause a Flame to Charge and break its tether.
FIREBALL SPLASH = 10 YARDS
Random raiders take 2,500–3,000 Fire damage, splashing everyone within 10 yards. Maintain assigned group spacing.
Melee Back Out If Fire Is Bad
Attack the Flames when positioning allows, but temporarily back away if Blaze or Eye Beam fire makes the area unsafe.
Both Flames Die · Illidan Lands · Main Tank Picks Him Up
Illidan tank immediately re-establishes control.
Transition healing from Flame tanks back to Illidan tank and raid.
Let the main tank establish Illidan before full damage resumes.
Phase 3 · Normal Form · 60 Seconds · Spread 5y+ · Agonizing Flames
NORMAL TANK CONTROLS ILLIDAN
Resume Phase 1 handling: Shear, Flame Crash, Draw Soul, and Parasitic Shadowfiend all continue.
AGONIZING FLAMES TARGET NEEDS COVERAGE
Keep the target alive through the 60-second DoT, while continuing tank and parasite coverage.
SPREAD MORE THAN 5 YARDS
Agonizing Flames hits a random player and everyone within 5 yards for 4,000 Fire damage, then pulses 3,000 Fire damage every 5 seconds for 60 seconds around the target.
PHASE 1 RULES CONTINUE
Stay behind Illidan, keep healing reduction active, move parasites to the designated area, and have ranged kill both spawns.
Phase 4 · Demon Form · 60 Seconds · WARLOCK TANK · Spread 5y+
WARLOCK TANK TAKES ILLIDAN
Demon Form is tanked at range by the Shadow Resistance-capped Warlock tank. The normal Illidan tank backs away and prepares for normal form to return.
EVERYONE STAY 15+ YARDS FROM ILLIDAN
Aura of Dread affects players within 15 yards: 1,000 Shadow every 2 seconds and 30% increased Shadow damage taken, making later ticks roughly 1,300. The Warlock tank also tanks outside it.
WARLOCK TANK ALONE
Shadow Blast hits for 8,750–10,750 Shadow damage and splashes everyone within 20 yards. No other player should stand near the Warlock tank.
LOCK HEALS ON WARLOCK TANK
Warlock tank healers maintain dedicated coverage through continuous Shadow Blast casts; other healers cover Flame Burst.
SWITCH ASSIGNMENTS ON FORM CHANGE
Heal the Warlock tank in Demon Form and return to the normal tank when Phase 3 resumes.
SPREAD MORE THAN 5 YARDS
Flame Burst hits every raider for about 3,200–3,500 Fire damage and splashes everyone within 5 yards for the same amount. Stay spread throughout Demon Form.
SHADOW DEMONS FIRST
Four Shadow Demons spawn at Illidan's feet. Each selects and stuns a random player marked by a purple beam; reaching that target instantly kills them, then the demon selects another target.
4 SHADOW DEMONS · INSTANT-KILL IF THEY ARRIVE
The stunned targets cannot kite. Pre-slow or pre-damage demons with Improved Blizzard or timed Seed of Corruption when available, then kill them immediately.
Normal Form · 60s ↔ Demon Form · 60s · Repeat Until 30%
Normal tank controls Phase 3; Warlock tank controls Phase 4.
Switch dedicated tank healing with each form.
Stay spread in both forms; Shadow Demons are absolute priority.
Phase 5 · Below 30% · P3/P4 Continue · Maiev Traps + Enrage
TRAP ILLIDAN = 15s DOUBLE-DAMAGE BURN
Drag Illidan onto Maiev's Shadow Trap. It stuns him and doubles damage taken for 15 seconds.
ENRAGE = DRAG INTO TRAP
Illidan's Enrage increases damage by 50% and attack speed by 30% for 20 seconds. Time movement so a Shadow Trap controls the Enrage whenever possible.
P3/P4 TANK SWAPS CONTINUE
The normal tank handles normal form; the Shadow Resistance Warlock tank remains required for every Demon Form below 30%.
EXPECT ENRAGE TANK SPIKES
Use heavy tank coverage if Enrage is active outside a trap, and keep switching between normal and Warlock tank assignments.
ALL PRIOR HEALING CHECKS CONTINUE
Cover Agonizing Flames and parasites in normal form, then Warlock tank Shadow Blasts and raid Flame Bursts in Demon Form.
P3/P4 CONTINUE BELOW 30%
Normal form retains Shear, Flame Crash, Draw Soul, Parasitic Shadowfiend, and Agonizing Flames. Demon Form retains 15+ yards from Illidan, 20+ yards from the Warlock tank, Flame Burst spread, and Shadow Demons.
TRAP ACTIVATES → BLOODLUST + COOLDOWNS
Maximize damage during the 15-second trap vulnerability; use Bloodlust/Heroism and offensive cooldowns according to raid timing.
Illidan Dies · Black Temple Clear
Keep Illidan controlled through the final burn.
Use remaining cooldowns.
Finish Illidan.

Wipe Causes
  • Shear lands, or Illidan faces the raid and Draw Soul heals him from multiple targets.
  • Healing reduction falls off before Draw Soul.
  • Tank or melee stand in Flame Crash fire.
  • A Phase 1 parasite is ignored or spawned parasites spread.
  • No Shadow Resistance Warlock tank is prepared for Demon Form.
  • Phase 2 groups are within 11 yards and Fireball splashes multiple groups.
  • A ranged player or healer leaves the center circle, causing a Flame Charge and broken tether.
  • A Flame breaks its Warglaive tether or a Flame tank dies.
  • A Flame faces the raid with Flame Blast, or its tank stands in Blaze.
  • Players cross Eye Beams or their fire trails.
  • Dark Barrage does not receive immediate focused healing.
  • Agonizing Flames remains stacked within 5 yards of the raid.
  • The Warlock tank enters Aura of Dread within 15 yards of Illidan.
  • Raiders stand within 20 yards of the Warlock tank and take Shadow Blast splash.
  • Raiders stack within 5 yards during Flame Burst.
  • The raid fails to switch tank and healer assignments between Phase 3 and Phase 4.
  • DPS fails to switch to Shadow Demons, or a demon reaches its stunned target.
  • The raid incorrectly assumes Demon Form stops below 30%.
  • Illidan misses Maiev's trap or Enrages outside it and kills the tank.
