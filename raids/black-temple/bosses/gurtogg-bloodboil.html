Tank Rotation · Face Gurtogg Away · Manage Acidic Wound
THREE-TANK ROTATION
Keep all three tanks high on threat. Rotate the active tank as Acidic Wound stacks become dangerous so previous tanks can let their stacks expire.
Face Boss Away From Raid
Arcing Smash and Fel-Acid Breath are frontal attacks. Keep Gurtogg pointed away from melee and the ranged group at all times.
Stay High on Threat
All rotation tanks maintain strong threat so the next tank can take control cleanly when the current tank needs relief. Bewildering Strike can disrupt the threat order.
Watch Acidic Wound Stacks
The active tank takes increasing Nature damage as Acidic Wound stacks build and armor is reduced. Expect tank damage to rise sharply before each rotation.
Swap Healing With the Tank
Follow the active tank rotation. Do not keep tunnel-healing a tank after Gurtogg has moved to the next tank.
Frontal Damage Is Avoidable
Only tanks should be in front of Gurtogg. Anyone else hit by frontal abilities is taking unnecessary lethal damage.
NEVER STAND IN FRONT
Stay behind or beside Gurtogg. Arcing Smash and Fel-Acid Breath make the front of the boss tank-only space.
Watch Threat During Tank Changes
Tank swaps can temporarily compress threat. Do not overtake the tanks while the rotation is being re-established.
Full Damage
Continue damaging Gurtogg while respecting tank rotations and Bloodboil positioning.
Bloodboil · Five Furthest Players · Rotate Groups
Keep Boss Stationary
Do not chase ranged players. Hold Gurtogg in a stable position so Bloodboil groups can manage distance consistently.
Maintain Rotation
Bloodboil does not stop the tank mechanics. Continue handling Acidic Wound and threat normally.
Bloodboil Hits the 5 Furthest
Each Bloodboil application targets the five players furthest from Gurtogg. Expect those players to take stacking periodic damage.
Rotate the Soak Group
Assigned ranged/healer groups rotate being the furthest players so one group does not accumulate excessive Bloodboil stacks.
Heal Current Bloodboil Group
Track which group is currently soaking and stabilize them while maintaining tank healing.
ONLY CURRENT GROUP STAYS FAR
The active Bloodboil group stays furthest from the boss. Other ranged players move slightly closer so the correct five players receive the debuff.
Rotate When Called
When raid lead calls the next group, the current group moves inward and the next group becomes the five furthest players.
Do Not Freelance the Distance
Random ranged players standing too far back can steal Bloodboil from the intended group and break the rotation.
Fel Rage · Random Player Becomes the Tank · HEAL THEM HARD
Boss Leaves the Tanks
During Fel Rage, Gurtogg focuses the selected non-tank raid member instead of the normal tank rotation. Tanks stop trying to force immediate control and prepare for Fel Rage to end.
Prepare to Regain Boss
Stay positioned and keep threat ready so Gurtogg can be picked up cleanly when Fel Rage ends.
ALL HEALERS → FEL RAGE TARGET
The Fel Rage player becomes Gurtogg's temporary target and takes extreme incoming damage. Immediately redirect heavy healing and keep them alive for the full duration.
Target Gets Huge Temporary Buffs
Fel Rage greatly increases the target's health and survivability, but they still require concentrated healing. Do not assume the buff makes them safe.
Resume Tank Healing After Rage
As Fel Rage ends, immediately transition healing back to the tank who regains Gurtogg.
IF YOU GET FEL RAGE — FIGHT
Stand your ground and fight Gurtogg. The Fel Rage target receives major temporary combat and survivability buffs and should attack the boss while healers keep them alive.
Everyone Else Keeps DPSing
Fel Rage is a burn window. Continue attacking while staying out of the boss's frontal arc.
Do Not Pull Boss Away
The Fel Rage target should remain in a controlled position rather than dragging Gurtogg through the raid.
Fel Rage Ends · Tanks Regain Control · Resume Rotation
PICK GURTOGG BACK UP
When Fel Rage ends, the tank group immediately re-establishes control and resumes the Acidic Wound rotation.
Check Existing Stacks
Do not blindly return to the tank who has the worst Acidic Wound stacks. Use the tank with the safest current stack state and strong threat.
SWAP BACK TO TANKS
The Fel Rage target no longer needs encounter-level focused healing once the phase ends. Re-lock healing onto the tank rotation immediately.
Stabilize Bloodboil Damage
Bloodboil damage and normal raid damage continue around the phase transition. Recover the raid while tank control is restored.
LET TANKS SETTLE
Give the tanks a moment to regain a clean threat order after Fel Rage before pushing maximum threat again.
Resume Normal Cycle
Return to normal positioning, Bloodboil rotation, and sustained damage until the next Fel Rage.

Rotate and Regain

Cover Every Target
Cover Bloodboil groups, tanks, and immediately collapse healing onto Fel Rage targets.
Position and Burn
Stay out of frontal attacks, obey Bloodboil distance rotations, and burn during Fel Rage.

Wipe Causes
  • Active tank holds Gurtogg too long and Acidic Wound stacks become unhealable.
  • Tanks lose the threat rotation and Gurtogg attacks a non-tank outside Fel Rage.
  • Boss is turned into the raid and frontal abilities hit melee or ranged players.
  • Wrong players stand furthest away and steal Bloodboil from the intended soak group.
  • Same Bloodboil group takes too many consecutive stacks.
  • Healers fail to switch immediately to the Fel Rage target.
  • Fel Rage target tries to kite instead of using the temporary buffs and fighting Gurtogg.
  • Tanks are not ready to regain the boss when Fel Rage ends.
  • DPS immediately overtakes tanks during the post-Fel-Rage transition.
  • Raid damage from Bloodboil combines with poor tank/Fel Rage healing and overwhelms healers.
