Phase 1 · 90 Seconds · Tank Rotation · Bloodboil Every 10s
KEEP ALL 3 TANKS HIGH ON THREAT
Maintain a controlled threat order. Bewildering Strike disorients the current primary-threat tank for 8 seconds, causing Gurtogg to ignore them and move to the next tank on threat.
ACIDIC WOUND · 60-SECOND DURATION
Each melee hit can add Acidic Wound, stacking Nature damage and Armor reduction. A tank needs roughly 60 seconds without new stacks for them to fall off.
Third Tank = Backup
Three tanks give the raid enough rotation depth for Acidic Wound downtime and forced Bewildering Strike swaps.
Frontal = Tanks Only
Arcing Smash and Fel-Acid Breath hit players in front. Arcing Smash reduces healing received by 50% for 4 seconds; Fel-Acid Breath deals initial Nature damage and applies a Nature DoT. Never turn Gurtogg toward the raid during a swap.
HEALING-INTENSIVE FIGHT
Expect heavy tank damage, stacking Bloodboil damage, and an intense 30-second Fel Rage healing window. Chain Heal and Circle of Healing are especially useful; extra hybrid healing may help if the raid is struggling.
Swap Healing With the Tank
Follow the active tank rotation. Do not keep tunnel-healing a tank after Gurtogg has moved to the next tank.
Frontal Damage Is Avoidable
Only tanks should be in front of Gurtogg. Anyone else hit by frontal abilities is taking unnecessary lethal damage.
NEVER STAND IN FRONT
Stay behind or beside Gurtogg. Arcing Smash and Fel-Acid Breath make the front of the boss tank-only space.
Watch Threat During Tank Changes
Tank swaps can temporarily compress threat. Do not overtake the tanks while the rotation is being re-established.
Full Damage
Continue damaging Gurtogg while respecting tank rotations and Bloodboil positioning.
Bloodboil · Every 10s · Five Furthest Players · Rotate 3 Groups
Keep Boss Stationary
Do not chase ranged players. Hold Gurtogg in a stable position so Bloodboil groups can manage distance consistently.
Maintain Rotation
Bloodboil does not stop the tank mechanics. Continue handling Acidic Wound and threat normally.
Bloodboil Hits the 5 Furthest
Bloodboil hits exactly the five furthest players every 10 seconds and applies a stacking DoT dealing 600 Physical damage each second for 24 seconds.
3 GROUPS OF 5
Split Ranged DPS and Healers into three groups of five. The active group steps farther back to become the five furthest players, takes one Bloodboil, then rejoins the clump.
Heal Current Bloodboil Group
Track the group soaking this cast and stabilize its stacking damage while maintaining tank healing.
Before Fel Rage: Spread Slightly
As the 90-second Phase 1 ends, Ranged DPS and Healers should loosen the clump slightly before the Fel Rage target is chosen—but not early enough to break Bloodboil control.
ONLY CURRENT GROUP STAYS FAR
Every 10 seconds, the active group steps out for one cast, then moves in as the next group becomes the five furthest players.
Melee Stay In
Melee normally stay behind Gurtogg and are not assigned to Bloodboil unless there are not enough Ranged DPS and Healers to form the soak groups.
Do Not Freelance the Distance
Random ranged players standing too far back can steal Bloodboil from the intended group and break the rotation.
Phase 2 · Fel Rage · 30 Seconds · KEEP TARGET ALIVE
Boss Leaves the Tanks
During Fel Rage, Gurtogg focuses the randomly selected raid member instead of the normal tank rotation. Tanks stop trying to force immediate control and prepare for Fel Rage to end.
Prepare to Regain Boss
Stay positioned and keep threat ready so Gurtogg can be picked up cleanly when Fel Rage ends.
FOCUS HEALING → FEL RAGE TARGET
The Fel Rage player must survive the full 30 seconds. Redirect intense healing and external cooldowns to them immediately.
Gurtogg Ramps During Fel Rage
Gurtogg deals 85% more damage and gains a stacking 8% melee-haste Fury buff while attacking the Fel Rage target. Healing pressure increases throughout the 30-second window.
Blessing of Protection Works
Blessing of Protection can be used on the Fel Rage target; Gurtogg continues focusing that player instead of switching targets. Pain Suppression is also excellent.
FEL RAGE TARGET MUST NOT DIE
If the target dies, Gurtogg returns to the normal threat table while retaining his Fel Rage damage and haste buffs, usually causing a rapid wipe.
IF FEL RAGE — STAND AND FIGHT
Hold a controlled position, keep Gurtogg facing away from the raid, attack him, and use every defensive or self-heal available. Do not kite him through the raid.
TARGET GETS MASSIVE BUFFS
Fel Rage grants +30,000 max health, +15,000 Armor, +300% damage, +100% healing done, and 50% reduced mana costs.
INSIGNIFICANCE = ZERO THREAT
Everyone except the Fel Rage target generates no threat during this 30-second Phase 2. Everyone else keeps DPSing; Bloodlust and major offensive cooldowns are ideal here if that matches the raid's strategy.
EVERYONE ELSE BACK AWAY
Fel Geyser erupts at the Fel Rage target's location and damages and knocks back nearby players. Give the target space, and remember Gurtogg's frontal attacks continue.
Fel Rage Ends · Tanks Regain Control · Resume Phase 1
PICK GURTOGG BACK UP
After 30 seconds, Insignificance ends and normal threat matters again. The tank group immediately re-establishes control and resumes the Acidic Wound rotation.
Check Existing Stacks
Use a tank with safe Acidic Wound status and appropriate threat rather than blindly returning to the tank with the worst stacks.
SWAP BACK TO TANKS
The Fel Rage target no longer needs encounter-level focused healing once the phase ends. Re-lock healing onto the tank rotation immediately.
Stabilize Bloodboil Damage
Bloodboil damage and normal raid damage continue around the phase transition. Recover the raid while tank control is restored.
LET TANKS SETTLE
Give the tanks a moment to regain a clean threat order after Fel Rage before pushing maximum threat again.
Resume Normal Cycle
Return to normal positioning, Bloodboil rotation, and sustained damage until the next Fel Rage.
Repeat Until Dead · 10-Minute Enrage
Rotate and Regain
Rotate Acidic Wound and regain control after every Fel Rage.
Cover Every Target
Cover Bloodboil groups, tanks, and immediately collapse healing onto Fel Rage targets.
Position and Burn
Stay out of frontal attacks, obey Bloodboil distance rotations, and burn during Fel Rage.
Initial Positioning
Initial positioning for the tank and Bloodboil groups.
Fel Rage Positioning
Raid positioning during Fel Rage.
Bloodboil Rule

Only the five furthest players get Bloodboil.

Current group OUT.

3 groups of 5 · rotate every 10s.

Wipe Causes
  • Acidic Wound stacks do not get enough downtime to fall off.
  • Tank threat order collapses after Bewildering Strike.
  • Boss is turned into the raid and frontal abilities hit melee or ranged players.
  • Wrong players stand furthest away and steal Bloodboil from the intended soak group.
  • Bloodboil groups fail to rotate every 10 seconds and one group takes repeated casts.
  • Fel Rage target is underhealed or dies; Gurtogg returns to normal threat while retaining his increased damage and Fury buffs.
  • Fel Rage target kites Gurtogg through the raid.
  • Players stand near the Fel Rage target and are hit by Fel Geyser.
  • Players stand in Gurtogg's frontal cones during Fel Rage.
  • Tanks are not ready to regain the boss when Fel Rage ends.
  • DPS immediately overtakes tanks during the post-Fel-Rage transition.
  • Raid damage from Bloodboil combines with poor tank/Fel Rage healing and overwhelms healers.
