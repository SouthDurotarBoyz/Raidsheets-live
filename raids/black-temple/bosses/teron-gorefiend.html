Pull · Stack the Living Raid · Leave Ghost Corners Clear
Hold Teron Still
Main tank picks up Teron Gorefiend and keeps him where he starts. This is a single-phase, single-tank fight with no swap.
Main Tank Cannot Get Shadow of Death
Shadow of Death targets other raid members, so the tank stays focused on threat and survival while the raid handles the ghost mechanic.
Extra Tanks
Only one tank is required. Extra tanks should contribute damage or healing if possible.
Living Raid Stacks Deep
Group the living raid tightly toward the back of the room while Teron remains near his starting position. This maximizes the distance Shadowy Constructs must travel.
Expect Random Shadow Damage
Doom Blossoms cast Shadow Bolt at random raid members throughout the fight. Keep the living group healthy and watch Crushing Shadows targets.
Keep Ghost Corners Clear
Shadow of Death players need the far opposite corners clear so their Constructs spawn as far from the living group as possible.
Full Damage
Outside Shadow of Death, stay on Teron and maximize damage.
Shadow of Death · Every 30s · 55s Until Death · Move at 15–20s
Keep Teron Stable
Do not move the boss after a player receives Shadow of Death. The affected player handles their own positioning while the tank keeps Teron controlled.
Heal Them Until They Leave
A Shadow of Death target continues fighting normally for most of the 55-second debuff. Keep them healthy until they move to the ghost corner.
Do Not Follow Them to the Corner
When the afflicted player runs out near the end of the timer, heal from range if possible. Do not drag the living raid toward their death location.
SHADOW OF DEATH = YOU WILL DIE
The debuff lasts 55 seconds. When it expires, you die, become a Vengeful Spirit, and spawn four Shadowy Constructs.
At 15–20 Seconds: Run to Corner
Keep fighting initially. With about 15–20 seconds remaining, move to the designated far corner so your four Constructs spawn as far from the living raid as possible.
DIE IN THE CORNER
Do not let Shadow of Death expire inside the raid. Your death location determines where the four Shadowy Constructs begin moving toward the group.
SOULSTONE SHADOW OF DEATH
When a player receives Shadow of Death, a nearby Warlock should Soulstone them so they can resurrect after completing their Vengeful Spirit job.
Vanish / Feign Death the Cast
Rogues can Vanish and Hunters can Feign Death after Shadow of Death begins casting but before it finishes. If timed correctly, the spell does not afflict them and their Constructs do not spawn.
Vengeful Spirit · KILL ALL 4 CONSTRUCTS BEFORE THEY REACH RAID
Living Tanks Cannot Fix This
Shadowy Constructs are the ghost player's responsibility. Keep Teron controlled and do not abandon the boss trying to chase Constructs.
Construct Reaches Raid = Emergency
If a Shadowy Construct reaches the living group it can begin attacking players for heavy damage. Keep the raid alive while the ghost finishes them.
Keep Healing Teron Damage
Doom Blossom and Crushing Shadows continue while the ghost player handles Constructs. Do not stop normal raid healing.
VOLLEY → CHAINS
Open immediately with Spirit Volley → Spirit Chains. Both have 15-second cooldowns; Chains immobilizes nearby Constructs for 5 seconds.
LANCE EACH CONSTRUCT — FARTHEST FIRST
Start with the farthest Construct and Spirit Lance each one once. Keep rotating targets and moving with the pack; Lance slows each Construct, but the slow gradually fades.
REPEAT UNTIL ALL 4 DIE
Keep rotating Spirit Lance until Spirit Volley and Spirit Chains are ready, then hit the grouped Constructs with another Volley → Chains. Keep them grouped so both abilities continue hitting all four.
After Your Constructs Die
Construct control comes first. If your four are dead and ghost time remains, help another ghost if needed. Then Spirit Strike can briefly reduce Teron's damage, or Spirit Shield can protect a living player from Shadow damage.
Incinerate + Crushing Shadows · Dispel Fast · Heal Shadow Damage
Maintain Threat
Teron's remaining mechanics target the raid. Keep him stationary and continue normal mitigation.
DISPEL INCINERATE NOW
Incinerate deals immediate Fire damage and applies a dangerous magic DoT. Priests and Paladins should Dispel Magic / Cleanse it immediately.
Watch Crushing Shadows
Players with Crushing Shadows take increased Shadow damage and are especially vulnerable to Doom Blossom Shadow Bolts. Prioritize them when damage spikes.
Doom Blossoms Keep Firing
Doom Blossoms are purple floating clouds that cast Shadow Bolt at random players. Keep the raid healthy through their damage.
Stay on Teron
Outside the Shadow of Death mechanic, stay on Teron and maximize damage.
Use Personal Defensives
If you have Crushing Shadows and take repeated Shadow Bolt damage, use Healthstones or defensive abilities instead of waiting at low health.
Repeat Until Teron Dies
Hold Teron Still
Dispel and Cover Damage
Dispel Incinerate and cover random Shadow damage.
Execute Every Ghost Cycle
Burn the boss and execute every Shadow of Death ghost cycle correctly.

Raid positioning for the Teron Gorefiend encounter.

  1. Spirit Volley

Wipe Causes
  • Shadow of Death player lets the debuff expire inside the living raid.
  • Shadow of Death player fails to move out with 15–20 seconds remaining.
  • Ghost player fails to control all four Shadowy Constructs.
  • Ghost fails to open with Volley → Chains or Lance all four Constructs.
  • Ghost tunnels one Construct while the others reach the raid.
  • Player does not understand the Vengeful Spirit action bar when they die.
  • Warlock fails to Soulstone a Shadow of Death player when available.
  • Incinerate is not dispelled quickly and the target dies to the Fire DoT.
  • Crushing Shadows target takes repeated Doom Blossom Shadow Bolts without enough healing.
  • Living raid moves toward the ghost corner and shortens the Constructs' travel distance.
  • Healers stop covering normal raid damage while a ghost player handles Constructs.
  • Too many Constructs reach living players and overwhelm the raid.
  • Raid DPS is too low and repeated encounter mechanics eventually overwhelm the raid.
