Phase 1 · ~1 Minute · Main Tank + Hateful Strike Tank · Avoid Molten Flame
Main Tank Holds Supremus
Main tank keeps Supremus stable and is excluded from Hateful Strike. The strike checks the 3 highest-threat players in melee range, then hits the eligible player with the highest current health.
Hateful Tank Must Stay Above Melee
Keep the Hateful Strike tank at higher current health than the melee DPS while they remain in the eligible high-threat group. Stay in melee range and high on threat.
Move Out of Blue Fire
Molten Flame spreads across the ground. Move Supremus only as much as necessary to keep tanks and melee out of the moving blue fire.
Keep Both Tanks Topped
Supremus hits the main tank while Hateful Strike repeatedly slams the second tank. Both tanks require heavy dedicated healing.
Hateful Tank Health Matters
Hateful Strike checks the 3 highest-threat players in melee range, excluding the current main tank, and hits the eligible player with the highest current health. Keep the Hateful tank above melee DPS.
Avoid Molten Flame
Blue fire is avoidable damage. Move immediately while keeping both tanks in healing range.
Stay Below the Hateful Tank
Melee must let tanks establish threat. Among the 3 highest-threat players in melee range, excluding the main tank, Hateful Strike hits the eligible player with the highest current health.
Melee in 3 Groups
Organize melee into three groups around Supremus so Molten Flame forces fewer players to move at once. Stay out of the fire and keep the Hateful tank safely eligible.
Ranged Loosely Spread
Ranged should stay loosely spread to reduce disruption from Molten Flame and maintain room to move.
Move Out of Molten Flame
Molten Flame targets a random player, tracks them for about 10 seconds while spreading along the ground, then the blue fire trail lingers for roughly another 10 seconds. Step out immediately.
Phase 2 · ~1 Minute · Fixate · RUN FROM SUPREMUS · Avoid Volcanoes
No Normal Tanking During Fixate
Supremus ignores normal threat while pursuing Fixate targets. Tanks spread with the raid and stay ready for the next Phase 1 pickup.
Supremus Moves Slower
During Fixate, Supremus moves more slowly than normal, giving the targeted player room to kite him safely. It is not safe to take a melee hit.
Prepare for Threat Reset
Phase 2 ends with a complete threat wipe. Move into position to pick Supremus up as Phase 1 begins.
Watch the Fixate Target
Supremus chases random raid members. Keep the fixated player alive while they run, but do not stand near Supremus to heal them.
Spread Across the Room
Phase 2 uses the full room. Maintain healing coverage while avoiding Supremus and volcanic geysers.
Avoid Volcanoes
Volcanic Geysers deal heavy Fire damage around them. Move away immediately.
IF FIXATED — RUN
When Supremus targets you, stop whatever you are doing and kite him away. Do not try to tank a melee hit.
Melee Can Keep Attacking
Melee may continue attacking while not fixated, but must immediately run when selected.
Close Target = Knocked Away
If Supremus selects a nearby player, he knocks them away first and then begins chasing them. Do not intentionally stand close.
Ranged Keep Distance
Ranged can continue damage once another target is selected, but should stay far enough away to safely react when Fixate changes. Watch for lingering Molten Flame at the start of Phase 2.
Avoid Volcanoes
Stay away from volcanic geysers while moving around the room.
Phase 2 → Phase 1 · COMPLETE THREAT RESET · Tanks First
Pick Supremus Up Immediately
Threat resets when Phase 2 ends. Any tank in position should establish control immediately, then settle back into the normal main tank / Hateful Strike tank setup.
Do Not Rip Threat
The threat reset can make large heals or active healing effects dangerous during the transition. Give the tanks a moment to establish control.
Re-lock Tank Assignments
Once Supremus is controlled, return immediately to main tank and Hateful Strike tank healing.
STOP FOR TANKS
Do not launch spells, refresh damage-over-time effects, or immediately resume melee as Phase 1 begins. Give tanks time to rebuild threat.
Resume Phase 1
Once tanks have control, return to full damage and repeat the cycle.
Repeat Until Dead
Repeat Positioning
Repeat main tank + Hateful Strike tank positioning.
Keep Both Tanks Healthy
Keep both tanks healthy and survive movement phases.
Respect Every Reset
Burn in Phase 1, kite safely in Phase 2, and respect every threat reset.
Supremus Positioning
Raid positioning for the Supremus encounter.
Wipe Causes
  • Hateful Strike tank falls below melee DPS in current health.
  • Melee DPS becomes an eligible Hateful Strike target.
  • Tanks lose control of the high-threat melee group.
  • Players stand in Molten Flame.
  • Fixated player does not immediately run.
  • A player is too close when Supremus switches Fixate and dies to melee.
  • Raid stands in Volcanic Geysers.
  • DPS resumes too early after the Phase 2 → Phase 1 threat wipe.
  • Healers pull threat during the reset.
  • Tanks fail to re-establish quickly after Phase 2.
