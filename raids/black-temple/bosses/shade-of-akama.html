Phase 1 · Kill Channelers · Control Both Doorways
Lock Down Both Doorways
Left and right tanks pick up the add waves spawning from their assigned doorway. Keep adds away from the Channelers and the raid.
Defender Tank Picks Up Defenders
Ashtongue Defenders hit hard and can interrupt casters. The Defender tank grabs them quickly and keeps them away from healers.
Do Not Chase Sorcerers
Ashtongue Sorcerers run directly to Shade and cannot be tanked. Hold your add assignment while DPS kills them.
Stay Away From Add Spawns
Position where you can reach assigned tanks without standing in the doorway paths. Do not let fresh adds reach the healer group.
Heal Assigned Add Tanks
Damage rises as waves accumulate. Keep left, right, and Defender tanks stable, especially if DPS is slow clearing the encounter.
MOVE OUT OF RAIN OF FIRE
Ashtongue Elementalists cast Rain of Fire on the raid. Move immediately instead of trying to heal through it.
KILL THE 6 CHANNELERS
The six Ashtongue Channelers are the primary Phase 1 objective. They continue binding Shade until killed.
SORCERER = IMMEDIATE KILL
Ashtongue Sorcerers periodically run to Shade and replace dead Channelers. They cannot be tanked. Swap and kill them immediately.
Control Extra Adds
If tanks become overloaded, crowd control spare Rogues, Elementalists, or other doorway adds instead of letting the waves overwhelm the raid.
Add Control · Interrupt Heals · Stay Out of Rain of Fire
Keep Adds Away From Channelers
Do not allow Spiritbinders or other adds to sit beside the Channelers. Keep your assigned pack controlled at the doorway side of the room.
Use Defensives if Waves Stack
If multiple waves accumulate, incoming damage can spike quickly. Use tank cooldowns rather than losing control of your side.
Interrupt Spiritbinder Heals
Ashtongue Spiritbinders can heal nearby enemies. Assigned kickers should stop their healing casts so progress on Channelers is not undone.
Watch Tank Spikes
Several adds can land attacks together. Do not assume an add tank is safe just because their health was stable a moment ago.
Spiritbinder Is the Dangerous Add
If the raid must kill a doorway add, prioritize Spiritbinders and stop their healing. Crowd control less urgent adds when possible.
Channelers and Sorcerers Stay Priority
Do not turn Phase 1 into a full trash clear. The encounter progresses by removing every active Channeler and replacement Sorcerer.
Phase 2 · Shade Is Free · EVERY DPS ON SHADE
DO NOT TRY TO TANK SHADE
Akama tanks his own Shade. Player tanks continue holding any remaining hostile adds and keep them away from the raid.
Survive Until Shade Dies
Do not abandon your remaining adds to chase the boss. Use defensive cooldowns if needed; all hostile adds despawn when Shade dies.
Keep Add Tanks Alive
Phase 2 is not safe just because Shade is active. Continue healing tanks that still have doorway adds or Defenders on them.
Akama Handles the Boss
The raid does not assign a player tank to Shade. Keep players alive while DPS races to finish the encounter.
EVERY DPS ON SHADE
Once Shade reaches Akama, stop killing normal adds and burn Shade of Akama immediately.
Bloodlust + Cooldowns
Use offensive cooldowns during the Shade burn. If Shade kills Akama before it dies, the encounter fails.
Ignore Remaining Adds
Tanks hold surviving adds while DPS finishes Shade. They despawn when Shade dies.
Shade Dies · Remaining Adds Despawn
Hold Surviving Adds
Hold surviving adds until they disappear.
Heal Through the Burn
Keep tanks alive through the final burn.
Finish Shade
Do not stop attacking Shade until it is dead.
Shade of Akama Positioning
Raid positioning for the Shade of Akama encounter.
Wipe Causes
  • A doorway is left uncovered and fresh adds run into the raid.
  • Ashtongue Defender reaches healers and interrupts or kills them.
  • DPS ignores a replacement Ashtongue Sorcerer and it resumes channeling on Shade.
  • Raid spends too much time killing normal adds instead of Channelers and Sorcerers.
  • Spiritbinder healing is allowed to go off and extends Phase 1.
  • Players stand in Elementalist Rain of Fire.
  • Tanks drag Spiritbinders close enough to heal Channelers or other priority targets.
  • Too many add waves accumulate and an add tank dies.
  • DPS continues killing adds after Shade is released instead of immediately burning the boss.
  • Shade kills Akama before the raid finishes the Phase 2 burn.
