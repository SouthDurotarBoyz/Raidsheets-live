Phase 1 · Kill Channelers · Control Both Doorways
Lock Down Both Doorways
Left and right tanks handle the Elementalist, Spiritbinder, and Rogue waves from their assigned doorway. A separate Ashtongue Defender spawns for the Defender tank.
Taunt the Kill Target
Quickly taunt and secure the first kill target from each doorway wave while crowd control handles the remaining mobs.
Channelers Need No Tank
The six Channelers only maintain the binding spell. They never attack players and do not need to be tanked.
Stay Away From Defender
Stay clear of add spawns and do not stand near the Defender; its melee-range Shield Bash can interrupt your casts.
Healing Focus = Add Tanks
Phase 1 healing is focused primarily on the tanks controlling the left and right waves, with assigned healing for the Defender tank and raid healing for incidental damage such as Rain of Fire.
MOVE OUT OF RAIN OF FIRE
Ashtongue Elementalists cast Rain of Fire on the raid. Move immediately instead of trying to heal through it.
KILL THE 6 CHANNELERS
The six Ashtongue Channelers continuously cast the binding spell around Shade. The assigned center team kills all six to release him.
DPS Split: Center + Doorways
Assign damage dealers between the six Channelers and the left/right add waves. Side teams kill Spiritbinders and control the rest while the center team burns Channelers.
No Threat on Channelers
Players assigned to the Channelers can attack freely; Channelers continue casting their binding spell and never attack the raid.
Add Control · Interrupt Heals · Stay Out of Rain of Fire
Keep Defender Away From Casters
Ashtongue Defender uses Shield Bash at melee range. Tank it away from healers and casters so it cannot interrupt them.
Keep Spiritbinders Away
Hold Spiritbinders away from the Channelers when possible so Chain Heal cannot undo the center team's progress.
CLEANSE DEBILITATING POISON
Rogue Debilitating Poison deals damage over time and slows attacks and casting. Cleanse it promptly.
Cover Incidental Damage
Keep assigned add tanks stable and heal raid damage from active Elementalists, especially Rain of Fire.
SPIRITBINDER = KILL TARGET
Kill the Spiritbinder from each doorway wave while crowd-controlling Elementalists and Rogues when possible.
Interrupt Spiritbinder Heals
Interrupt Chain Heal and other Spiritbinder healing. Chain Heal can undo progress on the Channelers; keep Spiritbinders away from them when possible.
CC Elementalists + Rogues
Use Polymorph or other suitable crowd control on Elementalists and Rogues so tanks do not have to hold the entire wave at once.
Interrupt Lightning Bolt
Elementalist Lightning Bolt deals ranged Nature damage and can be interrupted when the add is active.
Phase 2 · Shade Is Free · EVERY DPS ON SHADE
PLAYERS CANNOT TANK SHADE
Once the Channelers die, Shade moves to Akama. Akama tanks his own Shade; player tanks cannot take over the boss.
Survive Until Shade Dies
Do not abandon your remaining adds to chase the boss. Use defensive cooldowns if needed; all hostile adds despawn when Shade dies.
Keep Remaining Add Tanks Alive
Continue healing tanks holding surviving adds. DPS should be burning Shade rather than clearing those adds.
Akama Handles the Boss
The raid does not assign a player tank to Shade. Keep players alive while DPS races to finish the encounter.
EVERY DPS ON SHADE
Once Shade reaches Akama, stop killing normal adds and burn Shade of Akama immediately.
Kill Shade Before Akama Dies
Phase 2 is a burn. If Shade kills Akama before the raid kills Shade, the encounter resets.
Bloodlust + Cooldowns
Use Bloodlust and offensive cooldowns as an optimization for the Shade burn.
Ignore Remaining Adds
Tanks hold surviving adds while DPS finishes Shade. They despawn when Shade dies.
Shade Dies · Remaining Adds Despawn
Hold Surviving Adds
Hold surviving adds until they disappear.
Heal Through the Burn
Keep tanks alive through the final burn.
Finish Shade
Do not stop attacking Shade until it is dead.
Shade of Akama Positioning
Raid positioning for the Shade of Akama encounter.
Wipe Causes
  • A doorway is left uncovered and fresh adds run into the raid.
  • Ashtongue Defender is not picked up or reaches casters and Shield Bashes them.
  • Elementalist or Rogue crowd control is never established or breaks, overwhelming an add tank.
  • Insufficient DPS on the Channelers allows doorway waves to overwhelm the raid.
  • Spiritbinder healing lands repeatedly or heals the Channelers.
  • Players stand in Elementalist Rain of Fire.
  • Rogue Debilitating Poison is not cleansed promptly.
  • DPS continues killing adds after Shade is released instead of immediately burning the boss.
  • Tanks release surviving adds during Phase 2.
  • Shade kills Akama before the raid finishes the Phase 2 burn.
