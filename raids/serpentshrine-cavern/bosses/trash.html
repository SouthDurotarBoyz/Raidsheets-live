Pre-Karathress Trash

Underbog Colossus

Solo Kill
  • Pair 1: Acid Geyser + Serpentshrine ParasiteGeyser targets a random player; run immediately to the tank / Colossus position to avoid spraying the raid. The Parasite debuff eventually spawns a parasite that must be dealt with.
  • Pair 2: Atrophic Blow + FrenzyAtrophic Blow reduces Strength and Stamina; Frenzy increases attack speed.
  • Pair 3: Spore Quake + Initial InfectionMove quickly out of the very-large-range Quake. Initial Infection is a Nature-damage Disease; cleanse immediately.
  • Death effectsMay leave a Toxic Pool, spawn adds / Ragers, create a mana-regeneration mushroom, or do nothing.

How to handleUnderbog Colossus patrols alone and is killed as a solo pull. React to whichever ability pair it has and handle its random death effect. Move out of poisonous Toxic Pool. If adds / Ragers spawn when it dies, the Off Tank controls them quickly. Do not assume a single death outcome.
